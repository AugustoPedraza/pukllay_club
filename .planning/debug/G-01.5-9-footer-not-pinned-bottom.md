## Resolution

root_cause: |
  TWO INDEPENDENT CAUSES, on DIFFERENT PAGES, with DIFFERENT MECHANISMS and NON-OVERLAPPING
  fixes. This is not an AND-gate between them and not a duplicate of G-01.5-8 — it is one
  sentence of user report that turns out to have two separate true answers, and the UAT truth
  statement written under it describes only the first.

  ── CAUSE A — NO STICKY-FOOTER MECHANISM (site-wide; the UAT truth's literal subject) ──────
  `lib/pukllay_club_web/components/layouts/root.html.heex` renders `<body>{@inner_content}</body>`
  with no wrapper, and `Layouts.app/1` (`lib/pukllay_club_web/components/layouts.ex:641-656`)
  emits `<main>`, then `<.footer />` (the `<footer class="pk-footer">` at layouts.ex:1056), then
  `<.flash_group />`, as plain in-flow siblings. Measured live, `<body>` computes
  `min-height: 0px`, `display: block`, and a `height` exactly equal to its content; `.pk-footer`
  has no `margin-top: auto`; nothing on any shell element declares `min-height: 100vh`/`100dvh`
  or a flex/grid column. There is no mechanism by which the footer can reach the viewport bottom
  when the page is shorter than the viewport.

  It is an AND-gate with a page-state term, which is why it has shipped unnoticed:
    * TERM 1, the missing mechanism — present on 100% of routes, unconditionally, since the shell
      was written. Not a regression from this phase or any recent one.
