root_cause: |
  TWO confirmed contributing causes (AND-gated), sharing one upstream structural condition.

  UPSTREAM CONDITION (shared): `@media (min-width: 640px)` (app.css:2860-2868) holds BOTH of the
  Cierre band's distinguishing treatments — its `padding-block: 5rem` step AND its
  `font-size: clamp(2rem, 4vw, 3rem)` heading step. Mobile enters neither, so below 640px
  #cierre is styled as an ordinary `.pk-band` with an ordinary `text-2xl` heading, despite being
  the only band on the page whose content is two short text runs.

  CAUSE A — "it has too much space" (the band's padding-to-content ratio):
  `.pk-band { padding: 4.5rem 0 }` (app.css:2714) gives mobile #cierre a flat 72px/72px sized for
  content-rich bands. Below 480px, D-11 (`#cierre .pk-about-cierre-cta { display: none }`,
  app.css:5997-5999) then removes the Sumate button and one of the column's two gaps — 72px,
  **45.6% of the entire content column** — and no padding rule compensates. Measured at 375px:
  pad/content **1.67** against this page's own band norm of 0.19-0.53 and against this band's own
  human-ACCEPTED desktop state of 0.90-1.02. In rendered ink: 74.5px above and 73.5px below just
  82px of total content ink — **64.3% of the band is empty**. It is the only band on the page
  whose padding exceeds its content, by 67%.

  CAUSE B — "the tagline and 'Nos vemos el sábado' need better balance" (inverted visual
  hierarchy on the WIDTH axis): the band's three rendered lines measure **163.7 / 85.9 / 204.4px**
  — the widest object in the band is its smallest, greyest, most subordinate one. The 10px
  uppercase caption's box is **204.39px vs the 24px display heading's 163.73px: 24.8% wider**.
  This is mobile-specific because `#cierre h2`'s Cierre-only size step lives in the same 640px
  block, so Cierre's heading gets 2.00x the page-wide h2 norm at 1280px, 1.33x at 640px, and
  **1.00x — no step at all — at 375px**, putting the heading:caption type ratio at its page-wide
  minimum of 2.4:1 (vs 4:1 on desktop) at exactly the width where D-11 removes the button that
  had been separating the two elements.

  WHY BOTH ARE REQUIRED: no single-variable mutation moves both into range. Padding alone (S2)
  fixes A and leaves sig/h2 at 1.248; the heading alone (S6) fixes B and leaves pad/content at
  1.53. Only both together (C1) reach pad/content 0.85, sig/h2 0.936, symmetric 41.5/41.5px ink
  margins and 52.3% ink fill.

  NOT THE CAUSE (measured, see Eliminated): the 24px `gap` (tightening it makes A objectively
  worse — 1.85 at 16px, 1.95 at 12px), the caption's 10px override (raising it to 12px makes B
  worse — 1.498), the forced `<br>` (removing it makes both worse — 1.792 / 2.03), theme,
  viewport height, or any regression.

  TARGET RANGES (derived from this band's OWN human-accepted desktop state, NOT from taste):
    * pad/content -> 0.90-1.02. Reached by `padding-block: 2.5rem` (40px, ratio 0.93) at the
      current 86px column; `3rem` (48px) gives 1.12, slightly loose. If the heading step also
      ships, the column grows to ~94px and 2.5rem lands at 0.85 / 3rem at 1.02.
    * sig/h2 -> <= 1.0 (heading must out-measure its own caption). Reached at h2 32px
      (sig/h2 0.936, lines 218.3 / 85.9 / 204.4). h2 30px gives 0.999 — an exact tie, i.e. no
      hierarchy; prefer 32px. 32px is also exactly what the existing desktop clamp resolves to
      at 640px, so it continues the band's own size ramp rather than inventing a value.

  LANDMINES for the fix flow:
    1. THE RENDERED GAP IS 36px, NOT 24px. Bebas Neue is an all-caps face; "NOS VEMOS EL SÁBADO"
       has no descender ink, so 8.5px of its 32px line box below the baseline is permanently
       empty. 8.5 + 24 (declared) + 3.5 (caption leading) = 36.0px exactly. Anyone re-tuning
       this band by reading `gap: 1.5rem` off the stylesheet is reasoning from a number 12px

    2. DO NOT TOUCH THE GAP. D-12 requires one unconditional gap rule at every viewport, the

       tightening it degrades the very complaint it would be aimed at. This is now the THIRD

    3. A mobile `#cierre h2` size step must out-specify the `font-display text-2xl` Tailwind

    4. VERIFY NO HEADING WRAP AT THE NARROWEST SUPPORTED WIDTH. At 375px a 32px heading measures

       shrinks. A two-line heading would introduce a new defect.
