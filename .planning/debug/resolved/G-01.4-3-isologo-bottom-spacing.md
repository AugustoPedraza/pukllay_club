## Resolution

root_cause: |
  Three independently-necessary conditions (AND-gate confirmed):

  C1 MAGNITUDE — `mb-8` (32px) is 16.0% of the 200px mark's own height. The approved design source
  (sketch 045 variant A3, the recorded winner) measures 78.5px below a 180px mark = 43.6%. The
  shipped gap is 2.45x short in absolute terms and 2.7x short proportionally. The value was never
  derived: 01.4-06-PLAN.md picked it one step past the top of ui-design-system's documented spacing
  scale — a scale defined for text/section rhythm (max space-y-6 = 24px) with no tier for a display
  graphic — and its own human-check offered a correction ladder (mb-6/mb-10/mb-12 = 24/40/48px)
  whose largest option is still 39% below the design source.

  C2 INVERTED ALLOCATION — the hero's `py-12` puts 48px ABOVE the mark and only 32px below it
  (measured identical at 375/414/768/1280, both themes). The design source allocated 8px above and
  78.5px below — a below:above ratio of 9.8:1, versus the shipped 0.67:1, inverted ~15x. This is
  what makes the mark read as resting ON the text rather than merely close to it, and is what the
  user's word "balance" is pointing at. The sketch's clearance was the centring residual of a
  `min-height: 92vh; justify-content: center` hero; the implementation replaced that structure with
  a plain `py-12` block stack and never replaced the clearance the structure had been producing.

  C3 ZERO-CLEAR-SPACE ASSET — both isologo PNGs are tightly cropped: alpha ink reaches all four
  canvas edges at every threshold tested, and row coverage is still 31% of glyph width at 90.6%
  down and 20% at 93.8% down. The glyph is optically dense to its bottom edge and donates 0px of
  clear space, so the 32px margin (plus ~4px of the kicker's half-leading, ~36px ink-to-ink) is the
  ENTIRE perceived separation. A conventionally-padded brand lockup would have contributed 50-100px
  on its own at 200px. Any spacing intuition calibrated on padded logo assets under-specifies here.

  Compounding all three: the mark ships at 200px against an approved 180px (+11%), because
  01.4-05's anchor was deliberately sized as "180px mark plus breathing room" but the mark was sized
  from the anchor rect and consumed it; 01.4-06 then welded `--pk-about-mark-h: 200px` to both the
  anchor height and the mark height, so the anchor can no longer carry clearance by construction.
  The mark is larger than approved (needing MORE clearance) while getting 2.45x less.
