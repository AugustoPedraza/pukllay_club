## Resolution

root_cause: "`.pk-lightbox-img` (assets/css/app.css, `.pk-lightbox-img` rule ~line 3422-3436) declares a real, unconditional `height: 80vh`. Because its parent `.pk-lightbox` (~line 3357) is a `position: fixed; inset: 0` full-viewport flex container using `align-items: center; justify-content: center` (centers, does not stretch, its child), the remaining 20% of viewport height that the stage's own box does not occupy renders as two equal ~10%-of-viewport translucent-scrim bands, one above and one below the opaque stage, at every viewport size. This `80vh` value was deliberately carried forward unchanged from the previous round's `max-height: 80vh` cap by plan 01.2-28 (its own PLAN.md: 'Do NOT change the `80vh` number. It moves declaration but not value' / 'the lightbox keeps the exact vertical footprint a human already approved'), on the premise that reconsidering the number was a separate design decision — which is exactly the follow-up now surfaced as G-01.2-18."
fix: "Plan 01.2-29 replaced `.pk-lightbox-img`'s single `height: 80vh` with the file's own dual-declaration viewport-height idiom (already established at `.pk-app-shell`): `height: 100vh;` immediately followed by `height: 100dvh;`, adjacent and in that order, with no third `height` declaration. Every other declaration in the rule (width, background, object-fit, radius, shadow, inner scale-in comment, transform, transition) stayed byte-identical. The provenance comment above the rule was rewritten to record the centring-parent cause, the fired deferral condition, the `.pk-app-shell`-named mechanism, the considered-and-rejected percentage-of-parent alternative, and the scale-in's brief hairline inset."
verification: "Re-pointed the assertion in `test/pukllay_club_web/live/catalog_show_test.exs` to an ordered-pair regex match (RED committed first against the unchanged `80vh`, confirmed failing) plus a new count guard rejecting any third `height:` declaration (last-declaration-wins protection). Confirmed the built stylesheet `priv/static/assets/css/app.css` carries both lines after `mix assets.build`. `git diff --name-only -- lib/` empty for the whole plan. Full suite 645/645 passing, compile/format/credo clean. `.claude/skills/sketch-findings-pukllay_club/references/detail-page-mobile-interaction.md` corrected to show the shipped pair instead of the superseded `80vh`. Live-render confirmation (full-screen coverage with zero scrim band, mobile-toolbar tracking, close-button legibility in both themes, chevron/scale-in/corner-radius behavior against the taller stage) is pending human UAT — see `01.2-UAT.md` tests 19-23."
