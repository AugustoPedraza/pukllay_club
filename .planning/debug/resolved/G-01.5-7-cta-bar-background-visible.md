## Resolution

<!-- OVERWRITE as understanding evolves -->

root_cause: |
  Three contributing conditions, AND-gated (arm B proves no single one of the first two is
  sufficient; arm C proves the third becomes visible the moment the second is fixed):

  (1) DOMINANT - assets/css/app.css:2578, `.pk-about-cta-bar { border-top: 1px solid
      var(--color-base-300) }`. This hairline is the bar's ONLY declared boundary and measures
      1.415:1 light / 1.227:1 dark against the bar's own fill - far under the 3:1 WCAG 1.4.11
      non-text floor and under any perceptual threshold. Confirmed on rendered pixels at
      1.406:1 / 1.19:1.

  (2) CO-CAUSE - assets/css/app.css:2577, `.pk-about-cta-bar { background: var(--color-base-100) }`.
      base-100 is the token <html> itself paints as the page background, so over most of the page's
      scroll range the bar's fill is byte-identical (1.000:1) to what is behind it, and at the page
      bottom it is 1.155:1 / 1.086:1 against the base-200 Cierre band and footer. The fill
      contributes nothing, which is what makes (1) load-bearing. NOT independently fixable: the
      base-100/200/300 ladder's adjacent-step ceiling is 1.155:1 / 1.086:1.

  (3) THIRD CONTRIBUTOR, page-bottom only, latent until (1) is fixed - assets/css/app.css:5831,
      `body:has(.pk-about-cta-bar) { padding-bottom: 4.5rem }` = 72px against the bar's measured
      69px height, leaving a 3px strip of document background between the footer's bottom edge and
      the bar's top border.

  Net effect: the bar has neither a perceptible surface nor a perceptible edge. It is not
  transparent and nothing paints over it - it is opaque, correctly pinned, full-width, and paints
  the page's own background colour. "Background content visible behind the CTA" is literally what
  is rendered.

fix: "plan 01.5-10 (commit `e42d713`, `fix(01.5-10): give the sticky Sumate bar a real edge and re-derive its clearance (G-01.5-7)`): the dominant border-top channel was moved from `--color-base-300` to `--color-neutral` (the same lever arm E confirmed reaches 5.785:1 light / 7.128:1 dark), and the literal `4.5rem` clearance reservation was replaced with a live-measured `--pk-about-cta-bar-h` custom property tracking the bar's actual height, closing the AND-gate's dominant (1) and third-contributor (3) conditions this root cause named. The base-100 fill (co-cause 2) is left unchanged, per this root cause's own finding that it is not independently fixable within the base-100/200/300 ladder's 1.155:1/1.086:1 ceiling."
