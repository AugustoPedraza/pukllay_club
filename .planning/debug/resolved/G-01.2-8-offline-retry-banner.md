## Resolution

root_cause: "Two contributing causes act together to produce the reported symptom (AND-gate — both are required for the observed behavior; fixing only one still leaves the other truth violated): (1) [category: code/architecture] LiveView's `:more_error` inline retry-line (catalog_live/index.ex handle_event(\"load-more\")) is scoped to a live, connected round-trip that fails at the query level (safe_filter_games/1 returning :error) — it can only fire over an already-established websocket. A real network-offline condition (devtools throttled to offline) instead drops the underlying websocket transport itself before any load-more pushEvent can be delivered or replied to, which is a categorically different failure mode the custom retry-line was never built to catch; LiveView's own JS client independently detects the dropped socket and toggles the stock .phx-client-error/.phx-server-error classes. (2) [category: config/style] The flash_group/1 elements those classes trigger (#client-error, #server-error in layouts.ex) are verbatim, unmodified phx.new scaffolding — no app.css override of any phx-disconnected/phx-error/phx-loading/phx-client-error/phx-server-error selector exists anywhere in the codebase, no `pk-*` visual-identity class was ever applied to them (unlike every other component in this app), and their English gettext strings ('Something went wrong!' / 'Attempting to reconnect') were never localized to Spanish (no es/LC_MESSAGES catalog exists at all) even though the rest of the app is fully Spanish. This is why what surfaces is a generic daisyUI toast-top toast-end overlay in unbranded, untranslated boilerplate copy — 'the weird floating card at the top' — instead of either the app's branded inline retry line or even a themed/localized version of the connection banner."
fix: "Not applied — goal is find_root_cause_only. Suggested direction for the fix-designing step: (a) restyle/rebrand + localize the stock #client-error/#server-error flash elements (or replace their markup) so that IF a genuine transport-level disconnect occurs, what renders matches PukllayClub's visual identity (pk-* pattern) and is in Spanish; AND (b) treat the 'offline mid-scroll' UAT truth as describing behavior under a full transport disconnect, not just a scoped query failure — either accept that a true offline/socket-drop condition will always show the (now-rebranded) connection banner rather than the inline grid retry line (documenting this as an inherent LiveView architecture constraint, and adjusting the UI-SPEC's truth wording accordingly), or explore suppressing/hiding the default banner specifically while on the results-grid surface and layering the existing @more_error inline retry UI on top once LiveView's reconnection heuristics detect a live reconnect opportunity — the latter is more invasive and should be scoped carefully against LiveView's reconnect lifecycle before committing to it."
