Re-verified byte-identical sha256 output before and after the rename — pure naming/structure fix, no behavior change.
- **Files modified:** `tools/og-fallback/generate_og_fallback.py`
- **Verification:** Re-ran the generator twice (before/after), confirmed identical sha256 each time; ExUnit tagline guard's `Regex.run(~r/^TAGLINE_HEX\s*=\s*"(#[0-9A-Fa-f]{6})"/m, ...)` now succeeds against the real file.
- **Committed in:** `4abfe3d` (Task 2 commit, since the fix was required to make Task 2's own deliverable possible)
