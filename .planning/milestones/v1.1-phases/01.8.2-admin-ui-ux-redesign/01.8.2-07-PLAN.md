## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-01.8.2-25 | Spoofing | a disabled control that looks broken rather than empty, so staff cannot tell whether the app is stuck | medium | mitigate | D-24's render-time guard: `disabled` is accepted only with `commit={true}`, and the disabled paint uses muted stops so the label stays readable |
| T-01.8.2-26 | Spoofing | `--val` tint read as "this row is tappable" rather than "this is the datum you will change" | medium | mitigate | `editable_row/1` is the only component carrying the tint, its doc states the distinction, and `list_row/1` (tappable, untinted) is the contrasting case |
| T-01.8.2-27 | Information Disclosure | a status conveyed by colour alone, invisible to a colour-blind or screen-reader user | medium | mitigate | D-19h's dot-plus-word: the dot is decorative, the word is the accessible name, asserted by test |
| T-01.8.2-28 | Tampering | HEEx interpolation of a caller-supplied status or label into markup | low | mitigate | all values render through HEEx expression interpolation, which escapes by default; no `raw/1` and no hand-built HTML string anywhere in this module |
| T-01.8.2-29 | Denial of Service | a control under the 44px touch floor being unusable by tap on the primary platform | medium | mitigate | the floor is in every anatomy's CSS and is measured on the hit box; the geometry checks that markup cannot express are ported to plan 01.8.2-12's CDP harness |
</threat_model>
