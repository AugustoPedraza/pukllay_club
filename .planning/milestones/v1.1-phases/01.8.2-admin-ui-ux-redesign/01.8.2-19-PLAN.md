<objective>
The editor's field sheets: seven editable blocks each open a sheet that writes into the
page's draft, and the ⋮ menu that changes `status` outside it. This is where
*«la hoja PREPARA, el pie escribe»* becomes executable rather than a phrase.

Purpose: D-30 records two sheet patterns and says they are never mixed. The editor **field**
sheet is `.opt` + tick where choosing IS the commit and closes; the **draft** sheet (plan
01.8.2-20) is a classic form with one CTA. Text fields get their own wide `Guardar` inside
the sheet and choice fields do not — 079 found that closing a text sheet with ✕ silently
discarded what you had typed, which is a knowing amendment to 073 d41's "exactly one Guardar
in the editor". Getting this split wrong makes every field feel arbitrary.

Output: seven field sheets, the ⋮ status menu, and tests that a sheet never touches the
database.
</objective>
