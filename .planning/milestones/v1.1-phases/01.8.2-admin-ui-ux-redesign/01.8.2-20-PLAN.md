  <name>Task 1: open item 6 — how the live Publicar explains itself on tap</name>
  <decision>
**This is open item 6.** D-24's redraw of 078's P2 is decided — `Publicar` stays live and
explains on tap — but **no sketch shows what the explanation looks like**. CONTEXT says
planning either draws it or picks one and records the measurement.

The context: a draft with no `nivel` cannot be published, because `nivel` maps 1:1 onto
`sections.rule_value` and a nivel-less game would be invisible in every weight-band row.
`Publicar` is live; tapping it must say so. Three candidate shapes:

  **(a) A snackbar.** Tap → a snackbar reads the consequence («Elegí un nivel para que
  aparezca en las filas del inicio»). Cheapest; the message is transient and is not attached
  to the field it is about.

  **(b) An inline line under the gate.** Tap → a persistent line appears beneath `Publicar`
  naming the missing thing. Stays visible while the problem exists; costs vertical space in
  a sheet that already has a fixed CTA.

  **(c) The sheet scrolls to the nivel field and marks it.** Tap → the sheet scrolls the
  nivel row into view and marks it. Most direct — it puts the user where the fix is — and it
  is the only one that works when the missing field is off screen in the sheet.

Note what is already decided and not up for re-litigation: `Publicar` is **not** disabled
(D-24), the gate has exactly **one** condition and **none for an expansion** (D-30), and the
gate lives in `publish_game/1` as well as in the UI (D-37).

**Measure before choosing:** with the draft sheet open at 390x844 and at 360x640, is the
nivel row on screen when `Publicar` is at the foot? If it is off screen, (a) and (b) leave
the user to find it themselves.
  </decision>
  <options>
    - (a) snackbar
    - (b) inline line under the gate
    - (c) scroll to the nivel field and mark it
    - a combination — (c) plus a short snackbar — if the measurement shows the field is usually off screen
  </options>
  <recommendation>
Take the measurement first. If the nivel row is off screen at either width when `Publicar` is
at the foot, choose (c) — it is the only option that resolves "what is missing" and "where do
I fix it" in one tap, and D-24's whole argument is that the tap now carries the explanation.
If the field is always visible, (b) is the cheaper honest answer and keeps the message
attached to the state rather than to a moment.
  </recommendation>
  <acceptance_criteria>
    - the on-screen/off-screen measurement of the nivel row at 390x844 and 360x640 is recorded in the summary as a number
    - the developer's choice is recorded verbatim
    - the chosen shape is written into `01.8.2-UI-SPEC.md` as the rule for any future live-gate explanation, not only this one
  </acceptance_criteria>
  <resume-signal>After the on-screen/off-screen measurement is reported, choose: "a" (snackbar), "b" (inline line), "c" (scroll to nivel), or "c+a"</resume-signal>
  <done>Open item 6 is drawn: the explanation's shape is chosen against a measurement and recorded as a reusable rule.</done>
</task>
