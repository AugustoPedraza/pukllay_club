**Open item 1 — the real-device pass.** Every decision in sketches 069-080 was taken in
headless Chrome. CONTEXT calls this the single largest outstanding risk in the phase and
instructs that the device pass happen **before the UI slices harden** — which is here, with
six screen slices still unwritten.

Take a real phone (not an emulator, not CDP device emulation), sign in as staff, and work
through the shipped surface. Record what you find in
`.planning/phases/01.8.2-admin-ui-ux-redesign/01.8.2-DEVICE-PASS.md`, one line per finding,
each marked `OK`, `FIX NOW`, or `FIX IN SLICE <n>`.

What to exercise, and what specifically to watch for:
  1. **The press state (D-19o).** Tap every kind of control: a tab, a drawer row, an A1
     button, an A2 text action, an A3 icon, an A4 sheet row, a list row, a dashboard box.
     Does each visibly respond to the tap? **This is the one thing the harness cannot
     answer** — CDP touch emulation does not set `:active`, controlled for in D-19o by
     injecting a rule painted under `mouse.down()` and not under touch dispatch. A
     hover-only control is invisible to press on the primary platform.
  2. **The platform tap highlight.** Any flash of Android's Holo cyan
     (`rgba(51,181,229,0.4)`) anywhere means the `:root` suppression is not reaching that
     surface.
  3. **The drawer**, now on the left: does it open by tap, close by tap-outside, and is its
     edge-swipe behaviour sane against the browser's own back gesture?
  4. **The sheet**: does drag-down close it? Does the ✕ stay reachable when the sheet is
     taller than 85%? Does the keyboard push it or cover it (K1)?
  5. **The centred dialog**: is 312px right on a real 360px-wide screen? Is `Cancelar`
     actually the focused control?
  6. **The fixed foot save bar**: with the keyboard open, is `Guardar` reachable? Is the last
     block of the page hidden under the bar (D-28's stated failure mode for an estimated
     clearance)?
  7. **The tab bar** with the browser's own bottom UI present — this is real-world evidence
     for plan 01.8.2-10's D-14 decision, which was taken from headless measurements.
  8. **Reachability**: is anything important above the thumb's comfortable arc at the top of
     a 6.1" screen?

**Open item 4 — the keel.** Task 2 measured the shell's real content edges. Claude's
Discretion states a default of **16** (the admin's, matching D-19g-bis's 16/68 two-edge rule
and keeping d42/d46's R359 rule literal) against 079's **14** (which came from mirroring the
public ficha's container, and D-26 borrows the ficha's order and type, not its container).
CONTEXT requires the plan to state which was taken. Decide it now, against Task 2's numbers,
and record it.
