## Phase Goal

**As a** club staff member (the owner or one of up to 3 invited staff), **I want to** sign in and
manage the ludoteca, record where each physical game lives, curate the home page's sections, and fix
mis-banded games, **so that** Saturday operations run smoothly and the home page stays alive.

<objective>
Give staff the ludoteca editing surface: a searchable Juegos list, an edit screen for the
club-owned fields, and publish / retire / restore — plus the public-site affordances that take a
staff member straight from a game page to its editor.

Purpose: SC-2 ("staff can add, edit, and remove games") — this plan delivers edit and remove
(retire, D-08) on top of plan 02's lifecycle; plans 06-08 deliver add. D-34's Admin/Editar links
belong here because the edit route is the destination they need.

Output: `Game.admin_changeset/2`, admin read/write functions on `Catalog`, `Admin.GameLive.Index`
and `Admin.GameLive.Form`, the dashboard's Juegos card, and staff-only affordances on the public
header and detail page.
</objective>
