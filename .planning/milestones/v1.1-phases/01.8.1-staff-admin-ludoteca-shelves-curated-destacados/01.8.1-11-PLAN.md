## Phase Goal

**As a** club staff member (the owner or one of up to 3 invited staff), **I want to** sign in and
manage the ludoteca, record where each physical game lives, curate the home page's sections, and fix
mis-banded games, **so that** Saturday operations run smoothly and the home page stays alive.

<objective>
Finish moving the public site from hashtags to sections: the filter modal's facet, the `?sections=`
landing that section rows link to, and the game-page chips — implemented per the option the user
chose at plan 10's checkpoint.

Purpose: D-27 turns the tags facet into a sections facet; without this plan a "Spiel des Jahres"
section would exist on the home page but be unfilterable and invisible on its games' pages.

Output: `:sections` filter + facet options, `CatalogFilters` sections param, the `Secciones` pill
cluster, manual-section row links, chips per plan 10's option, and (option B only) the
`games.tags` drop migration.
</objective>
