## Phase Goal

**As a** club staff member (the owner or one of up to 3 invited staff), **I want to** sign in and
manage the ludoteca, record where each physical game lives, curate the home page's sections, and fix
mis-banded games, **so that** Saturday operations run smoothly and the home page stays alive.

<objective>
Harden add-by-BGG for real use: visible failure with Reintentar, duplicate rejection, URL paste,
expansions, and the production credentials the job needs.

Purpose: D-03's error UX and duplicate rule, and D-02's production secrets, are what turn plan 06's
happy path into something staff can rely on on a Saturday — and without the secrets every
production job fails (RESEARCH Pitfall 4).

Output: failure state + retry, `parse_bgg_input/1`, duplicate rejection, expansion-aware BGG
requests, deploy secret plumbing with a contract test.
</objective>
