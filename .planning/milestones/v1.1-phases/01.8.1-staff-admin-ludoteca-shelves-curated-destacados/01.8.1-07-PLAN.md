## Phase Goal

**As a** club staff member (the owner or one of up to 3 invited staff), **I want to** sign in and
manage the ludoteca, record where each physical game lives, curate the home page's sections, and fix
mis-banded games, **so that** Saturday operations run smoothly and the home page stays alive.

<objective>
Let the owner bring up to three staff members into the admin by email and take access away again,
with no public registration path (D-32, D-33).

Purpose: completes SC-1's invite-only model — plan 01 created the owner; this plan is how everyone
else gets in. The generator's unconfirmed-user + magic-link flow already is the invite (RESEARCH
Pattern 1), so no new token concept is introduced.

Output: `Accounts.invite_staff/2`, `list_staff/1`, `remove_staff/2`; `Admin.StaffLive.Index` at
`/admin/staff`; the owner-only dashboard card.
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| owner browser → invite/remove actions | Privileged account-management events |
| staff browser → `/admin/staff` | Authenticated but must not reach owner-only actions |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-01.8.1-32 | Elevation of privilege | invite/remove by a staff member | high | mitigate | Owner check in `invite_staff/2` and `remove_staff/2` (context) plus LiveView mount redirect; tested with a staff scope. |
| T-01.8.1-33 | Elevation of privilege | Removed staff keeping a live session | high | mitigate | Tokens deleted with the user and `UserAuth.disconnect_sessions/1` broadcast; second-conn test proves immediate loss of access. |
| T-01.8.1-34 | Denial of service | Owner removing themself / the only owner | medium | mitigate | `remove_staff/2` refuses non-staff targets and self. |
| T-01.8.1-35 | Spoofing | Invite sent to an attacker-controlled address | low | accept | Only the authenticated owner can invite; the cap of 3 bounds exposure; the owner can remove immediately. |
| T-01.8.1-36 | Repudiation | Who invited/removed whom | low | accept | ≤4 accounts, single owner — operational log via flash/app logs is sufficient at this scale. |
</threat_model>
