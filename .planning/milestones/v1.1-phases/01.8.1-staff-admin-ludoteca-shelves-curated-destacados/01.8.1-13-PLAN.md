## Phase Goal

**As a** club staff member (the owner or one of up to 3 invited staff), **I want to** sign in and
manage the ludoteca, record where each physical game lives, curate the home page's sections, and fix
mis-banded games, **so that** Saturday operations run smoothly and the home page stays alive.

<objective>
Show staff the games whose club difficulty band disagrees with BGG's weight and let them correct
or knowingly keep each one (SC-5, D-29, D-30) — and complete the dashboard.

Purpose: the CSV bands were not perfectly curated (expert-weight games sit in Ingenio estratega),
which misleads new players browsing by difficulty. The audit uses the reviewed thresholds from one
shared function so audit and any future use cannot disagree.

Output: `Vocabulary.implied_weight_band/1`, band-review columns, `Catalog.BandAudit`,
`Admin.BandAuditLive`, and the dashboard's Revisar niveles card completing D-35's order.
</objective>
