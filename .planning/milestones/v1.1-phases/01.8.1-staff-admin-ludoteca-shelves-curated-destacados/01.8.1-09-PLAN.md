## Phase Goal

**As a** club staff member (the owner or one of up to 3 invited staff), **I want to** sign in and
manage the ludoteca, record where each physical game lives, curate the home page's sections, and fix
mis-banded games, **so that** Saturday operations run smoothly and the home page stays alive.

<objective>
Record where every physical game lives: staff-managed shelves, a phone-first "walk the shelf"
tap-to-assign flow that the owner can spread over several Saturdays, and a pick/restore list in
walking order (SC-3, D-10..D-16).

Purpose: the one-time placement of ~434 games is the real cost (staff-admin-decisions.md), so the
assign screen is optimized for standing in front of a shelf with a phone: one tap, one save,
visible progress, instant move with undo.

Output: `shelves` table + `games.shelf_id`, `Catalog.Shelf`, `Catalog.Shelves`,
`Admin.ShelfLive.Assign` and `Admin.ShelfLive.Index` (management + pick list), the shelf select on
the edit form, and the dashboard Estantes card.
</objective>
