# Phase 01.8 Plan 02: Accessible Catalog Cover Names Summary

**A shared `PukllayClubWeb.GameText.cover_alt/1` generator now names every catalog and hover-preview cover surface — working image, broken image, and missing image alike — instead of two components each carrying six silent, icon-only failure branches.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** ~20 min
- **Completed:** 2026-09-12
- **Tasks:** 2 (Task 1: tdd, shared generator; Task 2: tdd, wired into six render branches)
- **Files modified:** 6 (3 created, 3 modified)

## Accomplishments
- `PukllayClubWeb.GameText.cover_alt/1` and `editorial_text/1` are the single generator for the D-10 accessible name — "Portada de [Juego]" alone, or "Portada de [Juego], editado por [Editorial]" — used by both `GameCard` and `GamePreview`.
- `editorial_text/1` is an independent nil-or-value function, mirroring `GamePreview.players_text/1`'s existing idiom, so a game with `publishers: []` (the schema default) never renders Elixir's list-inspect syntax or a dangling "editado por" with nothing after it.
- `GameCard.game_card/1`'s `<img>`, hidden broken-image `<div>`, and nil-cover `<div>` all carry the generated accessible name — the `<img>` via `alt`, both placeholder `<div>`s via `role="img"`/`aria-label`.
- `GamePreview.preview_body/1`'s equivalent three branches carry the identical treatment, so the hover-preview/mobile-sheet surface cannot drift from the resting card.
- `GameCard`'s moduledoc now records the reversal of its earlier "empty alt is intentional" decision, naming SEO-02/D-10 and the accepted redundancy against the visible `<h3>` caption.
- The `js-cover-fallback` hook class and the `hidden`/`flex` visibility contract both `assets/js/app.js` and `assets/css/app.css` depend on are untouched — only `alt`/`role`/`aria-label` attributes were added.

## Decisions Made
- Multiple publishers join with a plain `", "` (no "y"-conjunction special case for the last entry) — this catalog's publisher lists are short, so a plain comma join reads naturally; recorded in `GameText.cover_alt/1`'s `@doc`, per the task's discretion grant on this exact question.
- Reversed the `<img>`'s own empty `alt` (not only the two placeholder `<div>`s), per the plan's explicit instruction: D-10's name-plus-editorial form carries the publisher, information the adjacent `<h3>` caption doesn't provide, so the redundancy for the name portion was weighed and accepted rather than treated as disqualifying. The moduledoc paragraph that argued the opposite was rewritten in place, not deleted, to record the reversal and its reason.
- Precomputed `cover_alt` once per render into a `:cover_alt` assign in both components, rather than calling `GameText.cover_alt/1` three times inline — matches `GamePreview.preview_body/1`'s existing `:cover` precompute pattern.
