<objective>
Close G-01-5 (major): game expansions appear in the "Recién añadidos" carousel
row.

The diagnosis found an AND-gate with two jointly-necessary causes:
1. [data] The `Game` schema has no expansion column at all. The only signal is
   a free-text marker inside the club CSV's `Nombre` field, never parsed
   during seeding. `weight_band == nil` is not a safe proxy — it is also nil
   for genuine base games with no BGG id.
2. [code] `recent_query/0` applies zero filter and orders by `inserted_at`
   desc. Because the bulk seed inserts strictly in ascending `csv_row` order,
   that ordering collapses to "return the highest `csv_row` rows first" — and
   the club's source export happens to cluster every expansion and promo entry
   as one contiguous block at the tail of the sheet (`csv_row` 410-435 of 434).
   So the unfiltered recency row returns almost exclusively expansions.

Purpose: "Recién añadidos" is one of the eight curated D-09 shelves and is
currently showing a member a wall of expansion boxes for games they may not
own. This is the one gap in this closure round that is a data-model change,
not styling.

Output: a real `is_expansion` column, populated at seed time and backfilled by
the migration, and a recency query that excludes expansions.
</objective>

<ground_truth>
Do not re-derive this; it was established by the G-01-5 debug session and by
the club's own case-by-case review recorded in 01-UAT.md Test 5.

The expansion/promo set is exactly `csv_row` 410 through 435 — 26 rows, listed
verbatim in `priv/repo/seed_data/catalog_seed_report.md` under
`## Zero-hashtag rows` -> `### Unresolved`. The club reviewed every one of them
and confirmed all 26 are expansions or promos needing no independent weight
band.

- 22 of the 26 carry a text marker in `Nombre`: some form of `(expa`,
  `Expansión`/`Expansion`, or `Promo`.
- 4 carry no marker at all and were confirmed only by the club:
  `csv_row` 414, 415, 417 and 421 (the two "Viajes por la Tierra Media"
  sub-sets, "abyss leviatan", and "viniculture tuscany").

A marker-only parser therefore cannot be correct on its own — that is exactly
why G-01-5's `missing` list calls for "a manually reviewed list alongside the
parser". Both halves are required.

Also already checked, so you do not need to widen scope: all 26 of these rows
are in the zero-hashtag Unresolved set, meaning every one of them has
`weight_band == nil` and an empty `tags` array. The three weight-band carousel
rows and the four editorial-hashtag rows filter on exactly those columns, so
none of them can surface an expansion today. `recent_query/0` is the only
carousel query affected.
</ground_truth>

      independent ways for the two implementations to drift apart. Substring
      matching also removes any backtracking/ReDoS surface.

| club CSV export -> seed pipeline -> games table | Game names originate from a human-maintained spreadsheet outside the app and now drive a boolean classification decision |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-01-60 | Tampering | `add_games_is_expansion` migration SQL | high | mitigate | The backfill `UPDATE` is written entirely from literal values — no Elixir variable interpolation, no call into application code, no value sourced from the environment or a request. The predicate list is fixed at migration-authoring time, so there is no injection surface even though the statement is raw SQL. |
| T-01-61 | Denial of Service | `ExpansionClassifier.expansion?/2` | low | mitigate | Classification is plain downcased substring matching with a bounded marker list — no regex, therefore no catastrophic-backtracking surface — and it runs only at seed time and migration time, never on a request path. |
| T-01-62 | Tampering | production data backfill | medium | mitigate | The `UPDATE` only ever sets `is_expansion` and touches no other column; `change/0` supplies an inverting statement so the migration is reversible; and the automated verify runs a real `mix ecto.rollback --step 1` followed by a re-migrate before the task is accepted. Kamal's health-gated deploy means a migration failure leaves the previous container serving traffic. |
| T-01-63 | Information Disclosure | `games.is_expansion` | low | accept | The flag is derived entirely from data already public in the rendered catalogue (the game's own name) and reveals nothing about members, credentials, or infrastructure. |
| T-01-64 | Repudiation | scope of the exclusion | medium | mitigate | Silently hiding club-owned inventory from search would be an unlogged behavioural change.
