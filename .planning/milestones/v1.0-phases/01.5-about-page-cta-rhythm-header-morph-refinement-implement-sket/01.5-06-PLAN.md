<objective>
Give `.pk-band` ownership of its own outer spacing so the About page's content bands sit flush
against one another, and add the first geometric regression oracle this repo has for band adjacency.

Purpose: the UAT reported a visible whitespace strip between the tinted "Qué hacemos / Nuestra
historia" band and the dark FAQ band below it, contradicting D-14's promise that adjacent sections
separate by background colour alone. The debug session proved by differential test that the strip is
16px of `margin-block-end` contributed by the SHELL's `space-y-4` wrapper — not by anything the band
declares, not by band padding, and not by D-14. `.pk-band` declares 72px of padding and no margin at
all, so it never owned its outer spacing; the shell did, from another file, for every non-last child.

Output: one added declaration on the existing unlayered `.pk-band` rule with a comment naming what
it cancels and why; a recorded deliberate decision about the one boundary the band-scoped fix does
not reach; and a new developer-invoked CDP probe that measures whether adjacent band rects actually
touch.
</objective>

## What the diagnosis established (do not re-derive it)

`.planning/debug/inter-band-whitespace-gap.md` is a completed, evidence-backed root-cause session
with a reversible same-page-load differential test. Its findings are inputs, not hypotheses.

**Measured, at 1280x900, every direct child of `main > div.space-y-4`:**

| # | element | marginBottom | background |
|---|---------|--------------|------------|
| 0 | hero `div` | 16px | transparent |
| 1 | `section#fotos.pk-band` | 16px | transparent |
| 2 | `section.pk-band.pk-band-tint` | 16px | `rgb(243,236,250)` |
| 3 | `section#faq.pk-band.pk-band-dark` | 16px | `rgb(61,9,109)` |
| 4 | `section.pk-band` | 16px | transparent |
| 5 | `section#cierre.pk-band.pk-band-tint` | 16px | `rgb(243,236,250)` |

Every band computes `marginTop: 0px` and `paddingTop/Bottom: 72px`. All four band-to-band gaps
measure exactly 16px, identically at 390/768/1280px, in both themes.

**Differential test (decisive):** neutralising ONLY the `space-y-4` margin drives the gap from 16px
to exactly **0px** with no residual (document shrinks 96px = 6 non-last children x 16px, arithmetic
closing exactly). The control — zeroing `.pk-band`'s padding instead — leaves the gap at exactly
16px while shrinking the document 576px. The shell utility is the entire cause.

**Already eliminated — do NOT "fix" any of these:**

- `.pk-band` / `.pk-band-tint` / `.pk-band-dark` declaring their own margin. They do not:
  `.pk-band` is `padding: 4.5rem 0` and nothing else; the two modifiers declare background (and, for
  dark, colour) only.
- Band PADDING. Refuted by control experiment.
- A browser UA default `<section>` margin. Tailwind preflight zeroes it; a UA margin would show
  symmetric top+bottom, not the observed one-sided 16px.
- Missing full-bleed background. Both bands paint correctly and full-width; the strip is dead space
  between two correctly-painted boxes.
- A D-14 regression. `space-y-4` landed 2026-08-24 (`320fc4b`); `.pk-band-tint` landed 2026-09-08
  (`4d77558`). The gap predates D-14 by two weeks and exists at all four boundaries. D-14 is the
  EXPOSURE condition — it put saturated colour on both sides of one boundary — not the cause.
  **Reverting or retuning the tint would hide the symptom and leave the 16px everywhere.**

**Cascade fact that makes the fix deterministic:** the compiled stylesheet declares
`@layer theme, base, components, utilities;`. `.space-y-4` sits inside `@layer utilities` behind a
`:where(& > :not(:last-child))` selector contributing ZERO specificity. `.pk-band` sits UNLAYERED,
after every layer block closes. An unlayered rule beats a layered one regardless of specificity, so
the band reclaims its margin with no `!important` — this is the cascade-layer hazard `app.css`'s own
top-of-file note warns about, working in the fix's favour.

**Hard constraints on the fix (evidenced, not guesses):**

- Do NOT delete `space-y-4` from `layouts.ex`, and do NOT gate it on `fullbleed`. All three
  `Layouts.app` callers pass `fullbleed`, so `fullbleed` cannot discriminate, and
  `catalog_live/show.ex:292` documents that the catalog pages deliberately reproduce that exact
  16px gap.
- Do NOT treat this as a D-14 problem.
- The FIRST wrapper child is the hero `<div class="mx-auto ... space-y-6">`, which is NOT a
  `.pk-band` — so a band-scoped fix leaves hero→`#fotos` at 16px. The diagnosis requires this be
  decided deliberately rather than by omission (Task 1).
- `#cierre` is the last `.pk-band` and also carries 16px below it. Zeroing it removes 16px from the
  Cierre→footer distance, which overlaps G-01.5-3's item 4 — plan 01.5-08 owns the rest of that
  stack and already assumes this change.

**Why no existing gate caught it:** all four D-14 tests are CSS-source / class-attribute oracles —
they assert the bands have the right COLOURS. The bug is that they are not TOUCHING. Nothing in this
toolchain observes rendered geometry, which is what Task 2 exists to change.
</gap_context>
