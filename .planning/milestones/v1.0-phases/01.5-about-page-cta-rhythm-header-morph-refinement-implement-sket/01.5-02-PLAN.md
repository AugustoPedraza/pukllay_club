<objective>
Strip the Contacto card's chrome, redesign its contact links as soft accent-tinted chips that go
icon-only and circular on mobile, add Facebook as the third channel, and move the live Google Maps
embed out of Contacto and into Juntadas.

Contacto card reading as a second heavy "ask" competing with the closing CTA directly below it, and
found the map naming Juntadas' real-world meeting place while sitting under a heading about reach-out
channels. This plan resolves both, and closes the three-channel gap (Facebook exists in
`ClubLinks` and in `social_links/1` but was never surfaced on this page).

Output: edits to the Juntadas/Contacto `<section>` markup, a rewritten `.pk-about-contact-card` and
`.pk-about-contact-links` CSS block with a new ≤639px media block, an updated Contacto-links test
(3 links, not 2), a new map-containment test, and a one-line fix to the developer-invoked visual
probe.
</objective>
