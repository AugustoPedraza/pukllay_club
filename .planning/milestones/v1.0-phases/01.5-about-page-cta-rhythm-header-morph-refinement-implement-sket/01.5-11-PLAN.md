## What the diagnosis established — do NOT re-derive any of it

### The three channels, and which one is actually broken

| Gestalt channel | measured state | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| PROXIMITY | signature->button 29px ink vs signature->footer 84px/109px — a 2.9x-3.8x contrast pointing correctly UP | NOT broken. Differential P1 tiered the gap to 40/12 (a 3.3x contrast) and the dominant reading was unchanged. |
| COMMON REGION | band and footer paint 1.000:1, separated only by a 1.226:1 / 1.130:1 hairline | BROKEN — necessary half. S1/S2/S3 each restore a legible edge at ZERO geometry delta. |
| SIMILARITY | signature shares 5 of 7 computed type attributes with `.pk-footer-meta`, and 1 meaningless one (weight 400) with its own h2 | BROKEN — sufficient alone for the grouping sentence. T1 regrouped the tagline at zero geometry AND zero surface cost. |

**For the grouping sentence the two broken channels are an OR; for the report as written they are an
AND** — only the surface half answers "the bottom must be a different color". Ship both: three
consecutive self-verified sub-3:1 colour fixes have been human-rejected on this codebase, and the
type lever is the only channel whose effectiveness does not depend on a contrast ratio at all.

### Four things that are OFF LIMITS, each already tested

- **Do NOT re-open the external gap.** Differential S4 restores it and the symptom vanishes — and
  that 24px stripe is verbatim the thing the user reported as G-01.5-5 nine commits ago. Its value
  is diagnostic only: it is what identifies this as an oscillation on ONE boundary, not two defects.
- **Do NOT strip `#cierre`'s tint** (differential S3). It works mechanically and inverts the page's
  closing statement — the FOOTER becomes the only tinted band at the page's end — and it deletes
  D-14, which two prior sessions on this boundary each flagged as a trap.
- **Do NOT retune `#cierre .pk-band-inner { gap: 1.5rem }`.** It measures symmetric 24.00/24.00 and
  its ink asymmetry runs the other way. D-12's "one rule, unmodified at both viewports" is intact
  and this report is not evidence against it. (G-01.5-10 is a different gap_id and plan 01.5-13
  owns the mobile side — its diagnosis independently refuted this same rule a third time.)
- **Do NOT touch `#cierre`'s `padding-block: 5rem`.** It is painted band interior, symmetric by
  construction, held constant at 80.00px in every differential that removes the symptom, and it is
  G-01.5-6's deliberately-chosen value pinned by `checkCierreRunRatioBudget`.

### Why the footer's side, and why page-scoped

The equality has two sides. G-01.5-5's diagnosis ruled the band's side (D-14's tint) off-limits and
never considered the other one. `.pk-footer`'s `--color-base-200` is LOAD-BEARING on `/` and
`/juegos/1`: there the last in-flow child of `<main>` is a transparent `div.mx-auto.space-y-4`, and
that fill is the ONLY cue marking the footer at all. A global change would delete the footer's
identity on two pages that never reported a problem. `body:has(#cierre)` is already established four
lines from where this rule goes (app.css:5291), and `#cierre` exists on no other route.

### The oracle landmine (this is the part that bites if skipped)

`checkBottomBoundaryBudget` (`test/visual/about_geometry.mjs:720-765`) reads, literally: *when the
last band and the footer paint the identical token, REQUIRE them to be flush.* On the shipped tree
`surfacesMatch` is true and `distance` is 0.00, so it returns clean — it does not merely fail to
catch G-01.5-8, **it actively asserts the state G-01.5-8 is a report of.**

Change either fill and `surfacesMatch` becomes false, silently dropping this boundary into the
`else` branch's flat 26px budget — which would then permit a 24px gap to reappear here with a green
suite, quietly removing the contact guarantee G-01.5-5 was closed on. The rewrite is not optional
and it is not a re-run.

### Why no gate caught it

`checkAdjacentBandContact` iterates `section.pk-band` only, so it never reaches the footer. No
oracle in the repo compares two adjacent surfaces' backgrounds for DIFFERENCE, and none observes
type-register similarity between two elements. This is the sixth consecutive UI entry in this repo's
knowledge base whose gate asserted a value the defect satisfies — and the first where the gate
asserts the defect itself.

## The judgment calls this plan resolves, and on what evidence

### Footer fill: `--color-base-300`, page-scoped — with the runner-up recorded, not discarded

The diagnosis measured both available steps and both restore a legible bottom edge at zero geometry
delta. `--color-base-300` (S2, 1.226:1 light / 1.130:1 dark) is chosen because it is the LARGEST
step in this app's entire ladder for this pair and rendered most clearly, and because G-01.5-9's own
session independently corroborated the same change from a differently-worded complaint — which
slightly favours the larger step. `--color-base-100` (S1, 1.155:1 / 1.086:1) is recorded in the CSS
comment as the runner-up to reach for if the walkthrough says base-300 reads too heavy, so a
follow-up is a one-token edit rather than a re-diagnosis.

**Neither clears 3:1 and this plan does not pretend otherwise.** No pair in this app's ladder does,
in either theme; the user's literal request cannot be satisfied to the app's own documented standard
by any token swap. What makes the choice defensible rather than hopeful is the in-page control: the
SAME 1.155:1 step reads clearly at `#cierre`'s own top edge, in the same render, 338px higher up the
page — because it spans a 553px band meeting a 338px band rather than a 45px strip. Area matters,
and the page supplies its own control. This is exactly why the colour half is routed to the
end-of-phase walkthrough as a `<human-check>` rather than asserted closed.

### Keep the footer's `border-top` hairline as it is

On a real fill step the hairline stops being the sole boundary and becomes an ordinary underline.
Removing it would be a second, unrelated change inside the same edit; re-tokening it would be a
third. The same reasoning `search-right-align-mobile-cycle-2` recorded when it kept the hairline for
this exact token pair.

### Type register: colour only, and only at >= 640px

The diagnosis's T1 differential moved three attributes at once (size, case, colour). This plan moves
**one — colour — and scopes it to the desktop regime**, for two reasons that are about ownership,
not timidity:

- **Width is G-01.5-10's currency, and plan 01.5-13 spends it.** On mobile the signature's rendered
  width (204.39px) is one of the two operands in that gap's whole argument — its heading target
  (32px, 218.3px wide) was measured against exactly this signature. Raising the signature's size or
  weight here would move that operand under plan 01.5-13's feet; its own diagnosis already measured
  a size increase making the balance WORSE (sig/h2 1.248 -> 1.498). Colour moves no geometry at all.
- **The collision this gap is about is a desktop collision.** At >= 640px the two rules agree on all
  five attributes exactly. Below 640px the `<=639px` block already puts the signature at 10px with
  its own letter-spacing, so size and tracking already differ and the similarity channel is already
  partly broken there. Scoping to >= 640px means this plan and plan 01.5-13 touch the signature for
  two different reasons at two different widths, instead of both editing one rule — which is
  precisely what G-01.5-8's own diagnosis asked for in writing.

**The recorded next lever, if colour alone reads insufficient at the walkthrough:**
`text-transform: none`. It breaks a second collided attribute AND makes the line narrower, so unlike
every other option it helps G-01.5-10 rather than competing with it. Record it in the comment so the
follow-up does not have to re-derive the ordering.

### The oracle becomes a three-way rule, not an inverted two-way one

The diagnosis says to invert `surfacesMatch` from "require contact" to "fail". Taken alone that
would drop this boundary into the flat 26px budget and lose G-01.5-5's guarantee — the exact
landmine it warns about one paragraph earlier. The rule the design actually needs distinguishes
THREE cases, and the third is what keeps both gaps closed at once:

1. two adjacent full-bleed bands paint the identical computed background -> FAIL (no gap size is
   correct; this is G-01.5-8);
2. they differ and BOTH are opaque -> require CONTACT (any gap paints a visible third colour between
   two painted surfaces; this is G-01.5-5, and it is the state this plan ships);
3. the last band paints nothing at all (the catalog index and detail page's transparent trailing
   wrapper) -> the existing flat budget, unchanged.
</decisions_made>
