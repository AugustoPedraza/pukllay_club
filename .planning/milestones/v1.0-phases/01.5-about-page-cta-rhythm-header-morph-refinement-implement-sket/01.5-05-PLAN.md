<objective>
Fix the Sumate CTA's proportions at their single source: replace the raw one-axis height utility on
`Layouts.sumate_cta/1` with a real daisyUI size step composed with the project's touch floor, using
the exact composition `catalog_live/show.ex:502` already ships.

Purpose: the UAT reported the hero CTA as visually unbalanced against the isologo/wordmark lockup
(G-01.5-1) and, separately, the Cierre CTA as unbalanced too (G-01.5-3 item 5). The debug session
proved these are one defect: `min-h-12` stretches the button's height 35px -> 48px while daisyUI's
coupled `--btn-p` (16px) and `--fontsize` (14px) stay at the default step, collapsing the padding
ratio to 1.03:1 — geometrically a square-padded label box, not a button. Every alternative measured
sits at 1.78-2.00:1. The class is also an ORPHANED CONSTRAINT: quick task 260822-2v9 added it purely
to match a 48px theme-toggle anchor in a desktop header row (`.pk-nav-actions`) that no longer
exists anywhere in `lib/`.

Output: one changed class list on one component, one updated component test, and a recorded design
decision on the two open questions the gap left open (which size step, and whether this CTA adopts
the sketches' pill radius).
</objective>

## What the diagnosis established (do not re-derive it)

`.planning/debug/hero-cta-isologo-balance.md` is a completed, evidence-backed root-cause session.
Its findings are inputs to this plan, not hypotheses to re-test.

**Confirmed, measured:**

| variant | box | font | h-pad | v-pad | h:v pad ratio |
|---------|-----|------|-------|-------|---------------|
| shipped (height utility) | 85.41 x 48 | 14px | 16px | 15.5px | **1.03 : 1** |
| height utility removed | 85.41 x 35 | 14px | 16px | 9.0px | 1.78 : 1 |
| `btn-lg` (no height utility) | 108.09 x 42 | 18px | 20px | 10.5px | 1.90 : 1 |
| design-source spec | 116.75 x 48 | 16px | 28px | 14.0px | 2.00 : 1 |

The shipped CTA is byte-identical at 375/430/768/1280px in both themes, i.e. it is the only hero
object that does not scale, and at 85.41px it is the narrowest visible object in the hero — narrower
than the 111.19px companion wordmark and 52.3% of the isologo's 163.45px.

**Already eliminated — do NOT "fix" any of these:**

- Horizontal mis-centering. Refuted by measurement: mark/wordmark centre vs CTA centre differ by
  0.23px at 375px and 0.23px at 1280px. Sub-pixel.
- The isologo's `--pk-about-mark-clear` being half-consumed by the absolutely-positioned companion
  wordmark. Real mechanism, but production's wordmark->eyebrow ink gap (36px) is LOOSER than both
  sketches (10.56px / 21.56px), so it cannot explain the report.
- The hero column's flat `space-y-3`. Demoted to a secondary amplifier: both sketches use a flat
  16px hero gap and the human approved sketch 051's hero composition as-is. The size-step mutation
  fixes the report with the 12px stack left untouched.
- The 4px `--radius-field`. Demoted to secondary (the "kind" channel). Genuinely open as a DESIGN
  question (Task 1), but not required to explain the report.

**The AND-gate fires on the FIX, not the cause.** One cause is sufficient, but a size step alone
lands at 42px in this app (`--size-field: 0.21875rem`), 2px under the documented 44px touch floor —
so the size step MUST be composed with the floor utility, exactly as `catalog_live/show.ex:502`
already does. Shipping the size step bare would trade a design defect for an accessibility one.
