<objective>
Fix the lightbox's contrast in dark theme, give it a soft open and close, and finish its keyboard
navigation.

The reported symptom is one line in each UAT gap — "the lightbox doesn't contranst with background
page" on mobile, "the overlay lightbox need polished" on desktop. Grounding it in the real stylesheet
turned it into a specific bug rather than a taste question: the backdrop mixes this app's text colour
into the scrim. In light theme the text colour is near-black, so the scrim reads as normal dimming and
the bug is invisible. In dark theme the text colour is near-white, so the "dimming" scrim is a pale
veil laid over an already-dark page — the exact opposite of what a lightbox backdrop is for.

The same wrong value is written a second time on the mobile preview sheet's backdrop, and the
lightbox's own comment records that the two are deliberately one value. So this is one fix declared
once, with two readers, not two independent patches.

Two smaller finishes ride along, both from the same sketch round: the open and close are an instant
display switch against an app whose motion language is otherwise soft, and the prev/next chevrons that
already exist in the markup have no keyboard equivalent.

Purpose: a member who taps a cover image in dark theme should see the photo, not a washed-out page.
Output: one scrim declaration with two readers, a fade-and-scale open/close, arrow-key navigation, and
a written decision on where the arrows anchor.
</objective>
