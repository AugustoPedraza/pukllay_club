<objective>
Replace the one surface in this app that still ships raw generator boilerplate.

`flash_group/1`'s `#client-error` and `#server-error` elements are verbatim, untouched `phx.new`
scaffolding: a stock daisyUI `toast toast-top toast-end` fixed overlay, carrying no `pk-*` class
anywhere unlike every other component in this codebase, in untranslated English ("Something went
wrong!" / "Attempting to reconnect" / "We can't find the internet") even though the rest of the app
is Spanish and no `es` gettext catalog exists at all. Under a genuine devtools-offline condition the
websocket transport drops before any `load-more` push can reach the server, so the app's own
`:more_error` inline retry line is architecturally unreachable and LiveView's own client detects the
dropped socket and shows this instead — "a weird floating card at the top."

Sketch 030 resolved the design with variant A, **Inline Bar**: a full-width bar directly under the
header, in the app's own accent tint rather than its danger colour, centred, in one combined Spanish
line. A restyled corner toast and a compact retry-line-shaped pill were both tried and lost.

There is a second, non-cosmetic half to this gap. `01.2-UI-SPEC.md` wrote a truth ("scrolling to
trigger a page load shows the compact inline retry line") that the architecture cannot satisfy under
a real transport disconnect, and no test tooling could have caught that. That wording gets corrected
here, in the same plan, so the next round of UAT tests something that can actually happen.

Purpose: a member whose connection blips should see this app, not a Phoenix generator.
Output: an on-brand Spanish connection bar, the stock toast markup gone, and a UI-SPEC that
distinguishes a failing query from a dropped socket.
</objective>

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-01.2-15-01 | Spoofing | A persistent "Reconectando…" bar could be mistaken for a system-level prompt | low | mitigate | The bar carries no input, no link and no action — it is a `role="status"` announcement only. It cannot solicit anything from the member, so it cannot be leveraged into a credential or action prompt. |
| T-01.2-15-02 | Injection (XSS) | The bar's message | low | mitigate | The message is a static literal in the template with no interpolation. Assert with `grep` that the bar's markup contains no `{...}` interpolation and no `raw(`. |
| T-01.2-15-03 | Information Disclosure | Deleting the client-vs-server error distinction | low | accept | The distinction leaked a small amount of server-health information to any visitor. Collapsing to one message discloses strictly less, not more. |
| T-01.2-15-04 | Denial of Service | The bar showing during a flapping connection | low | accept | Visibility is driven entirely by LiveView's own connection lifecycle, which this plan does not modify. No new timer, listener or round trip is introduced. |
| T-01.2-15-SC | Tampering | npm/pip/cargo installs | high | mitigate | Not applicable — this plan installs no package from any registry, and deliberately does not add a gettext catalog dependency either. `git diff mix.lock assets/package-lock.json` must be empty at the end of the plan. |
