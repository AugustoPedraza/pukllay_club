<objective>
Make the top of the mobile game-detail page read as one thing instead of three, and make the dot
indicator look like a dot indicator.

UAT test 8 reported three separate observations against the same 390px view: the carousel dots "have
too much space between them — this should be a default 3 dots"; the pills, the image and the CTA read
as "too many floating stuff"; and "the margin left and right of the shell isn't the same for all the
components".

Grounding all three in the stylesheet turned them from taste into geometry. The dots are 44px-square
hit boxes wrapped around 8px marks, so ~40px of the distance between two marks is invisible tap area.
The pills row and the poster panel are siblings with no shared container, so only the photo gets a
card. And the panel adds its own 1rem of card padding that the pills row, being outside it, never
carried — which is exactly why the photo's edge looks 16px further in than everything else.

Two of the three resolve with one move: put the pills inside the panel they already sit directly
above. They keep their approved position, they gain the card, and they gain the same inset the photo
has.

Purpose: the phone's buy-box should read as one decision surface with one edge.
Output: a compact dot row with an intact tap target, and a single bordered card holding the pills, the
photo and the dots.
</objective>
