Sketch 055 — dark-mode primary-as-text contrast fix

Quick task 260910-efe, Task 3 checkpoint. Sketch 054's winner (W2 Deep Jewel, #8C2BB6) is excellent as a FILL (6.70:1 white-on-primary) but fails as TEXT — 2.34:1 against the new --color-base-100, below the 4.5:1 WCAG AA floor. This sketch compares the real candidates against the real affected elements.

Full-surface finding (not in the original checkpoint): the 17 affected rules sit mostly on --color-base-100 (header, drawer, pill-tag, dropdown panel — all confirmed base-100 in app.css), so base-100 is the surface that matters for this fix. But checked against all three ladder tones for completeness: neither W1 nor W3 (Option C) clears 4.5:1 on base-200 AND base-300 — W1 fails both, W3 fails base-300 only. Only the ink-token approach (Option A) clears all three unconditionally. See the matrix below.
