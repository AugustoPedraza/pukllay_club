★ Winner — E1: Icon-Only, Centered

Icon-Only, Centered

The drawer's bottom block ends on two quiet rows: social links lead (bare icons, full color, first — the more important of the two, per developer feedback), a hairline divider, then Tema trails as three small centered icon buttons with no label, no container, and no row shape of its own — mirroring exactly how social itself carries no visible text. Discoverability rests on the monitor/sun/moon icons plus each button's aria-label, the same contract social already ships under everywhere else in the app.

Measured footprint: divider + social + divider + Tema icons is 136px — 21.3% of the drawer's height, down from the first working version's 183px / 28.7%.

How this was reached

  1. Round 1: found the real bug — the shipped .pk-drawer-utility uses justify-content: space-between, pinning the "Tema" label to the opposite edge from its own buttons. Compared bare-icon vs. segmented-track vs. binary-switch component shapes; bare icons won.
  2. Round 2: the fixed version still measured 28.7% of the drawer's height — too heavy. Tried merged-row, one-label, and micro-eyebrow refinements.
  3. Round 3: picked micro-eyebrows, but both rows used the identical pattern and read as "two sections of the same thing." Differentiated social (content, first, bare) from Tema (control, last, contained) by shape.
  4. Round 4: the container itself became the heaviest shape on the page. Dropped it for a full-width row borrowing the nav-links' own rhythm. Also confirmed (with a real toggle) that the drawer correctly covers the header when open, matching the shipped app.
  5. Round 5: the full-width row reintroduced the exact same space-between bug from Round 1. Fixed by tightly grouping the label with its icons; added a divider between social and Tema.
  6. Round 6: "Tema" still read as overloading even grouped and muted. Reframed it as a small centered drawer-footer instead of a list row at all — three alternatives built and compared; icon-only (no label) won.
