Final answer to "how do A (tight pill) and B (flat, no chrome) combine, with real tap affordance?": purely informational pills (facts/mecánicas/temáticas/editorial) use flat no-chrome text — nothing to tap, no border needed. Interactive chips (filters) use A's tighter sizing, with an always-visible border at rest (not just on hover) — the real industry pattern for signaling tappability when there's no hover state to lean on (Material "filter chip", Airbnb's own filter pills) — plus a genuine tap-press feedback (scale + flash) on :active.
