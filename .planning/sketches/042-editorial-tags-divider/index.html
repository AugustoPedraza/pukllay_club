Sketch 042 — full round history (A-G, rounds 1-6) in this sketch's README. Ghost Chip won over the pill (round 4) and over three body-scale, more "social-hashtag" treatments (round 5). Round 6 tightened the tag row's surrounding gap from 32px to 24px — but a negative-margin-on-top-of-gap technique broke the rendered layout ("killed the design"). Round 7 (this version) fixes it with a plain, deterministic spacing model instead: no shared flex `gap`, every section sets its own explicit margin-top. Toggle 🌙/☀ to check both themes.
What changed (round 7, the fix): round 6's approach — a shared .text-col { gap: 32px } plus a negative margin on one child to fake a smaller gap — visually broke.
