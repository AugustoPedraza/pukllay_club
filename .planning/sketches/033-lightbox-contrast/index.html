Why this needed fixing, not just restyling: production's lightbox backdrop was color-mix(in srgb, var(--color-base-content) 55%, transparent) — it mixes the text color into the scrim, not a background color. In light theme that's a dark color (looks fine); in dark theme --color-base-content is near-white (#F3ECFA), so the "dimming" scrim was actually a pale veil over an already-dark page — the opposite of what a lightbox backdrop needs. This fixes it with a fixed dark value that never inverts, matching the --pk-shadow-color pattern this app already uses for every other floating surface (sheets, drawers, popovers).
