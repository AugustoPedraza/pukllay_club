## D-14 Measurement — pre-fix (Task 2 checkpoint, recorded verbatim from the developer's resumed answer)

Taken in real headless Chrome, real magic-link login as the owner account, `getBoundingClientRect()`/z-index at 390×844 and 360×640 on `/` and `/juegos/326`, before any Task 3 code existed:

1. **Index page: no collision.** `.pk-filter-trigger` sits at y 9.5–53.5 at both viewports; the tab bar at y 777–844 (390×844) and y 573–640 (360×640). `filterTriggerCoveredByTabBar: false` at both. CONTEXT.md's "pk-bottom-collapse navbar, filter controls" framing does not correspond to a real bottom-fixed element in the shipped catalog UI — `bottom_collapse` is a padding mechanism, not a second bar. This corrects the premise D-14 was originally framed on; the correction is recorded in `01.8.2-UI-SPEC.md`'s new "Tab bar" section, not just here.
2. **Detail page: full occlusion, not partial.** Both bars are `position: fixed; bottom: 0` with no coordination — they overlap rather than stack. Tab bar z-index 70, top 777 / bottom 844 (390×844), top 573 / bottom 640 (360×640). CTA bar z-index 45, top 775 / bottom 844, top 571 / bottom 640. The reserve button itself (top 788 / bottom 832 at 390×844; top 584 / bottom 628 at 360×640) is **entirely inside** the tab bar's band at both viewports. `overlapPx: 67` at both. `ctaBtnFullyCoveredByTabBar: true` at both.
3. **Visible content heights:** collide-as-overlap 711px (390×844) / 507px (360×640); collide-as-stack 644px / 440px; merge-or-suppress 713px / 509px.
4. **Filter reachability under any option:** unaffected — 1 tap, same as today, since it was never bottom-anchored.
