<task type="checkpoint:decision">
  <name>Task 1: open item 5 — the three copy choices the developer owns</name>
  <decision>
Three admin copy choices were recorded open on purpose (CONTEXT `<open_items>` #5). Each
needs a decision before the slices that render the strings are written.

**1. "agregado" vs "ya tiene sus datos".** Sketch 077 counted *"agregado"* said twice about
one game meaning two different things — creation (the game row now exists) and arrival (BGG
enrichment finished and the game has its data). Pick one word for each, or one phrasing that
separates them.

**2. `Ver` vs `Editar` on the completion toast.** When enrichment finishes and the snackbar
offers an action, does it read `Ver` (look at the result) or `Editar` (go change it)?

**3. `Guardar` vs `Guardar cambios`.** D-00a and 065 R7 #3 settled on the one-word
**`Guardar`** for the editor's save bar; 01.8.1's shipped copy says `Guardar cambios`
(01.8.1-UI-SPEC.md L144). Confirm the one-word form, or keep the longer one.

**Pre-resolved by project standard, not open:** the `+` sheet's hint currently uses tuteo
(`hasta que lo publiques`) against the admin's voseo everywhere else. That is a defect
against this project's Rioplatense/voseo standard, not a question — it is fixed to voseo
in Task 3 regardless of the answers above.
  </decision>
  <options>
    - Answer all three now, and the answers are written into `01.8.2-UI-SPEC.md`'s copywriting contract in Task 3
    - Defer any of the three, in which case Task 3 records it as an explicit open item carried into UAT (D-21) rather than a slice choosing one
  </options>
  <recommendation>
Take `Guardar` (one word) — D-00a already settled it and 065 R7 #3 measured it; keeping
`Guardar cambios` would reopen a closed decision. The other two are genuinely the
developer's, since both turn on how the club talks about its own catalog.
  </recommendation>
  <acceptance_criteria>
    - the developer's answer to each of the three is recorded verbatim in the plan summary
    - any deferred item is named explicitly as deferred, never silently defaulted
  </acceptance_criteria>
  <resume-signal>Answer each of the three: (1) "agregado" vs "ya tiene sus datos", (2) Ver vs Editar, (3) Guardar vs Guardar cambios — or say "defer" for any one</resume-signal>
  <done>Three copy choices are decided or explicitly deferred, in the developer's own words.</done>
