## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-01.8.2-70 | Tampering | a stale slot index committing a copy into the wrong physical position after a concurrent reindex | high | mitigate | the index is re-validated inside the locked transaction; a changed estante aborts the commit and surfaces D-11's quiet snackbar rather than landing the copy silently |
| T-01.8.2-71 | Tampering | a move implemented as remove-then-place, leaving the copy nowhere if the second step fails | high | mitigate | the move is one `place_copy/3` transaction under two locks taken in ascending `shelf_id` order, asserted by a context test that a rolled-back move leaves the old estante unchanged |
| T-01.8.2-72 | Spoofing | Quitar del estante confirmed as a sheet row, reading as one more option rather than a decision | high | mitigate | D-19f's centred dialog, asserted by a test that the dialog is not nested inside a sheet |
| T-01.8.2-73 | Tampering | a client-supplied `copy_id` or `shelf_id` acting on a copy the staff member is not looking at | medium | mitigate | every write goes through the `Shelves` context, which re-reads the copy inside the transaction; the route is staff-gated and there is no cross-tenant model in this app |
| T-01.8.2-74 | Denial of Service | the drop animation running for a user who asked for no motion | low | mitigate | the animation is gated behind `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)`, following this repo's established idiom, pinned by a grep |
