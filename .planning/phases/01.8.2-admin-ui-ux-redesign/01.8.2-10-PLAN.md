<task type="checkpoint:decision">
  <name>Task 2: D-14 / open item 2 — collide or merge, decided by measurement</name>
  <decision>
**This is open item 2, and it has no sketch.** D-14 names sketch 074 as what settles it, but
that number was taken by `074-admin-header`; the staff-tab-bar-on-public-pages sketch was
never made at any number. CONTEXT records D-14 as still genuinely open and instructs that a
slice must not silently pick one.

The question: on a public catalog page viewed by signed-in staff, the 5-tab staff bar (67px)
now shares the bottom of the screen with the catalog's own `pk-bottom-collapse` navbar and
its filter controls. Do the two **collide** (both render, stacked, costing ~130px of a
390x844 viewport) or **merge** (one bottom surface, with the catalog's controls folded into
or replaced by the tab bar on that viewport)?

**Measure first, then choose.** Before answering, Task 1's bar is on screen; measure and
report, at 390x844 and at 360x640:
  - visible content height with both bars rendered (collide)
  - visible content height with the tab bar only (merge)
  - whether the catalog's filter control is still reachable in the merge option, and in how
    many taps
  - whether the tab bar overlaps the detail page's `.pk-mobile-cta-bar` (the reserve CTA),
    which is itself a fixed bottom surface

The measurement goes in the summary as numbers. The choice goes in `01.8.2-UI-SPEC.md`.
  </decision>
  <options>
    - **Collide** — both bottom surfaces render. Honest, no behaviour changes for the catalog, costs the most vertical space.
    - **Merge** — one bottom surface on public pages for staff; the catalog's own bottom controls fold into it or are suppressed while the staff bar shows.
    - **Suppress on specific public surfaces** — the staff tab bar hides on the pages that already own the bottom (the catalog index's filter bar, the detail page's reserve CTA) and shows everywhere else. A third option the measurement may make obvious.
    - **Sketch it first** — stop here, run `/gsd-sketch`, and bring the answer back. Open item 2 lists this as the alternative.
  </options>
  <recommendation>
Take the measurement before forming a preference — the whole reason this is open is that
nobody has measured it. If the numbers do not clearly separate the options, prefer the third
option (suppress where another surface already owns the bottom): it keeps D-13b's promise on
the pages where the staff bar is genuinely useful without fighting two shipped fixed bars
whose geometry other phases already tuned.
  </recommendation>
  <acceptance_criteria>
    - the four measurements above are recorded in the summary as numbers, at both viewport widths
    - the developer's choice is recorded verbatim
    - the choice is written into `01.8.2-UI-SPEC.md` as a rule, not left in the summary alone
    - if the choice is "sketch it first", the plan records that and Task 3 is deferred rather than guessing
  </acceptance_criteria>
  <resume-signal>After the four measurements are reported, choose: "collide", "merge", "suppress", or "sketch it first"</resume-signal>
  <done>D-14 is answered from measured numbers and the answer is written into the UI spec.</done>
