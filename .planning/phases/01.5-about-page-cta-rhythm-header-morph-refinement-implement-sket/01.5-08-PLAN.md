<objective>
Close the last open piece of G-01.5-3: the empty band between the closing band and the footer. Opt
the About page into the bottom-boundary mechanism the repo already built for this exact defect, and
relocate the mobile CTA bar's clearance from an in-flow spacer that does not work to the end of the
document where it does.

Purpose: the UAT reported "the bottom has bottom space from viewport" and a distinct empty band below
the Cierre content. The debug session enumerated every in-flow node between the band and the footer
and closed the arithmetic to exactly zero residual: there is no single cause and nothing orphaned —
the About page's bottom boundary simply STACKS five (≤480px) / three (≥481px) independently-reasonable
declarations owned by four decisions in three files. That is a named, already-solved defect class in
this repo. `Layouts.app`'s `bottom_collapse` attr was built for it, its own CSS comment records the
catalog page's identical measured stack, and both catalog callers opt in. `about_live.ex` passes
neither it nor `boundary_collapse` — an opt-in that was never opted into.

Separately, `.pk-about-cta-spacer` does not do its job: the fixed Sumate bar still overlays the
footer at page bottom, because the spacer sits before the footer in flow. Its 72px is dead space that
contributes to the reported void without buying the clearance it was added for. That is a placement
question, not a height retune.

Output: one attr on one `Layouts.app` call, the spacer replaced by page-scoped body clearance using
an idiom this file already documents, a contract test that surfaces the next caller to forget, and a
bottom-boundary budget oracle on the geometry probe.

## What the diagnosis established (do not re-derive it)

`.planning/debug/cierre-band-whitespace.md` enumerated every in-flow node between `#cierre`'s bottom
edge and `<footer>`'s top edge and isolated each contributor by differential test.

**The exact chain (measured, zero unexplained residual):**

| contributor | ≤480px | ≥481px | owner |
|-------------|--------|--------|-------|
| shell inter-child margin, on `#cierre` | 16px | 16px | `layouts.ex` — closed by plan 01.5-06 |
| `.pk-about-cta-spacer` height | 72px | — | `app.css` (hidden above 480px) |
| shell inter-child margin, on the spacer | 16px | — | `layouts.ex` |
| `<main>`'s default bottom padding | 80px | 80px | `layouts.ex` |
| `.pk-footer`'s own top margin | 16px | 48px | `app.css` |
| **total** | **200px** | **144px** | |

Neutralising all four contributors drives the distance to exactly **0px** at both widths. The
control — zeroing `.pk-band`'s padding instead — leaves it completely unmoved. The largest single
contributor is `<main>`'s bottom padding (40% mobile / 56% desktop); the spacer the objective flagged
as the likely culprit is 36% on mobile and exactly **0%** above 480px, yet the void is 144px at every
width ≥481px.

**Simulating `bottom_collapse` at runtime** (zero `<main>` bottom padding + the collapsed footer
margin) measured 144 → **24px** at 768px and 200 → **112px** at 390px. The residual 112px on mobile
is the spacer's 72px plus its margins, which the attr does not and should not know about — that is
Task 2's job.

**Why it reads as a distinct BAND rather than trailing page space (exposure condition, AND-gate
YES):** the whole strip is unpainted and shows the page background, sandwiched between two surfaces
painted the SAME token — `#cierre`'s D-14 tint above and `.pk-footer`'s own background below.
Stripping ONLY the tint at runtime, geometry untouched, leaves all 200px in place yet makes the void
merge into a continuous run and the "extra band" reading vanish. **Do NOT revert or retune the
tint** — that hides a real defect and deletes a deliberate decision.

**The spacer does not work.** `.pk-about-cta-bar` is fixed at the viewport bottom, 73px tall (69px
once plan 01.5-05's size step lands), always covering the bottom of the screen.
`.pk-about-cta-spacer` is 72px of in-flow height placed BEFORE `<footer>`. Measured scrolled to page
bottom: the bar covers the footer, visible in
`.planning/debug/assets/cierre-band-whitespace/mobile-390-page-bottom.png`. The spacer clears the
Cierre band from the bar, which nothing needed, and fails to clear the footer, which is what the bar
actually overlays.

**Already eliminated — do NOT "fix" any of these:**

- A duplicate or leftover spacer element, or stray padding. Every direct child of the shell wrapper
  was enumerated; all ten are accounted for and intentional. The two elements after the spacer are
  `position: fixed` and contribute zero height.
- Band PADDING. Refuted by control experiment.
- Treating the spacer as the primary cause. It is 0% of the void above 480px.
- A D-14 regression.

**Hard constraints on the fix:**

- Do NOT touch the shell's inter-child spacing utility or `<main>`'s own bottom padding directly.
  Both are load-bearing for the catalog pages — `catalog_live/show.ex:292` documents depending on the
  16px — which is precisely why the opt-in attr exists.
- Do NOT assume "mobile". Item 4 IS worse on mobile (200 vs 144) but exists at every width. Any fix
  must be checked in all three regimes: ≤480px, the ungoverned 481-639px middle band, and ≥640px.
- `boundary_collapse` also owns the TOP boundary. The About page's top spacing is not in question, so
  the bottom-only attr is the correct one; picking the wrong attr silently moves the hero.

**Why no gate caught it:** there is no test anywhere in the repo asserting anything about the
distance from the last band to the footer, and no contract test asserting that a `Layouts.app`
caller has considered its bottom boundary at all.
