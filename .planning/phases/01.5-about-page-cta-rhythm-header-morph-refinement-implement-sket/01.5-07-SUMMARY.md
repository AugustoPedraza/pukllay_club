## Decisions Made

### Task 1 checkpoint: reduce-floor, ~70% viewport height

**Chosen:** `reduce-floor` at 70% (the plan's recommended starting fraction, confirmed by the developer as-is).

**Rationale (relayed from the developer):** Keep D-10's "distinct closing moment" intent — the close should still feel taller and more deliberate than a plain 72px band — but reduce the floor so the content (heading, button, signature) fills a more believable share of the band instead of the ~17% fill the shipped 100vh floor produced (the exact "huge space top and bottom" the UAT reported).

**Rejected options:**
- `keep-full-viewport` — leaves the exact proportion problem the UAT flagged; only fixes the asymmetry (Task 2's unconditional padding removal), not the volume.
- `drop-floor` — simplest (band falls to its natural ~220.7px content height, identical to every other band's shared padding recipe), but gives up D-10's destination intent and supersedes a decision made deliberately earlier in this same phase.
- `add-content` — addresses the proportion problem at its other end (more content to justify the height), but is new copy/design work, explicitly out of scope for a gap-closure round.

**Status of D-10:** REVISED, not superseded. The desktop-only full-viewport-style treatment survives in reduced form; only the exact fraction and the now-unneeded header-height compensation changed.

### Unconditional fix (not part of the checkpoint decision)

The header-height top-padding compensation (`padding: var(--pk-header-h, 4.5rem) 0 0`) was removed regardless of Task 1's answer, since the diagnosis proved it was shifting the true (even) gap split by exactly one header height at every width and viewport height measured, and its premise (the band contrasting visually against the fixed header) no longer held after plan 01.5-04/D-14 painted the band the header's own tint.
