  suite.
- **Every plan's own `<self_check>`** (recorded in its SUMMARY.md) confirmed its threat model's
  cited test files exist and its commits landed, before that plan was allowed to complete.
- **No threat exceeds `high`** — nothing rated `critical` appears in the register.
