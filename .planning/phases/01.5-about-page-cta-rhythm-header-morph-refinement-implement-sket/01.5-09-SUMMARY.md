## Decisions Made

- **`padding-block: 5rem`** for `#cierre` at >=640px, chosen over the 6rem runner-up: puts the inbound run 5.6% above the page's own rhythm (vs. 6rem's 17%), and the band's distinctness as a closing moment is carried by its tint (D-14) and larger heading (`clamp(2rem, 4vw, 3rem)`) rather than by padding magnitude alone. Runner-up (6rem, run 168px, band 366px, 47.5% fill) is recorded in the CSS comment for a one-value retune if an end-of-phase walkthrough finds 5rem cramped.
- **Symmetric padding KEPT**, optical re-centring declined: the debug session's asymmetric-padding option was reasoned against the OLD page, where a 24px unpainted stripe still sat below the band. After G-01.5-5 closes that stripe, the band's bottom edge stops being a visual boundary against the footer at all, changing the arithmetic the option was based on. Carried forward as an observation for the next UAT round to judge against the now-fixed page, not acted on here — also because asymmetric padding would fail the closed `checkCierreGapEvenness` oracle and reopen 01.5-07's closed decision.
- **Cierre-to-footer target is 0px**, not a smaller budget: all three other band-to-band boundaries on the page measure exactly 0.00px, and the UAT truth explicitly asks for the band "flowing directly into the footer" — flush is this page's established language, and this boundary was its one exception.
