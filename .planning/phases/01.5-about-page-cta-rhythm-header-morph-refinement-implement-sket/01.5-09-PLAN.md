## What the two diagnoses established — do NOT re-derive any of it

Both sessions ran live measure-mutate-restore differentials on the running dev server via CDP, with

### G-01.5-5 — the Cierre-to-footer stripe

**Not a regression, not a missed width, not an unaccounted element.** A 10 width x height sweep plus
dark theme measured the distance at exactly **24.00px at every width >=481px** (560/768/1280/1366/
1440/1512/1920) and exactly **16.00px at 390px** — precisely the budget plan 01.5-08 declared, with
zero variance, invariant to viewport height and theme. The full in-flow node walk between `#cierre`
and `<footer>` finds only `position: fixed` or zero-height nodes; `#cierre`'s margin-bottom is 0,
`<main>`'s padding-bottom is 0. One declaration owns 100% of it with zero residual:
`assets/css/app.css:5163-5166`, `main.pk-bottom-collapse + .pk-footer,
main.pk-boundary-collapse + .pk-footer { margin-top: 1.5rem }` (and its `1rem` sibling inside the
`@media (max-width: 480px)` block near line 5691).

**The defect is EXPOSURE, not size — an AND-gate of two individually-correct declarations:**

- Kill the footer's own top margin alone -> distance 24.00 -> **0.00**, the two tinted surfaces
  become one continuous band separated only by the footer's existing 1px `border-top` hairline, and
  the footer's own `.pk-footer-row` 24px inner padding still insets its content. Symptom gone,
  geometry changed.
- Strip `#cierre`'s tint alone (geometry untouched) -> distance stays **24.00**, every pixel
  survives, and the "whitespace band" reading vanishes anyway. Symptom gone, geometry unchanged.

Measured surfaces at the boundary: `#cierre` `rgb(243,236,250)` light / `rgb(34,16,58)` dark;
`.pk-footer` **byte-identical** in both themes; `<body>` transparent; `<html>` paints the gap
`--color-base-100`. The stripe is a third colour isolated between two same-token surfaces, which is
why the eye reads its presence rather than its height.

**Working-case control (the discriminating variable, geometry held constant):** the catalog index
and detail pages measure the SAME 24.00px at the SAME boundary from the SAME declaration and have
shipped unreported for two phases — because their last in-flow child of `<main>` is
`div.mx-auto.space-y-4` with `background-color: rgba(0,0,0,0)`. About is the first and only
`Layouts.app` caller whose last in-flow element paints a full-bleed background, and paints it the
footer's exact token.

**Why the target is 0 and not a smaller number:** all four band-to-band boundaries on this page
measure exactly **0.00px** — flush is this page's established language (plan 01.5-06), and the
Cierre->footer boundary is its single exception. It is also the only boundary on the page whose two
surfaces are the same colour. A shorter stripe is still a stripe.

**Not asymmetry.** `gapTop - gapBottom` is exactly **0.00px** at 10 widths x 4 heights x 2 themes,
and was ALSO exactly equal (193.00/193.00, 228.00/228.00, 238.67/238.67) in the pre-dfb1259 state the
user actually reported on. UAT coverage test 8 genuinely passes. The word "lopsided" in this gap's
own truth statement is inherited from the superseded G-01.5-3 item 3b and is now false.

**Not a regression from dfb1259, and not "70vh is still too generous".** The `min-height: 70vh/70dvh`
mechanism no longer exists in source or compiled CSS; band height is a flat 430.00px across viewport
heights 720/800/900/1080, which no vh floor can produce. An in-page historical reconstruction of the

complained-of quantity by 34-56% and removed its viewport-height coupling. It landed 4h23m AFTER the

**The live defect is the absolute magnitude of one chosen constant.** `assets/css/app.css:2769-2772`,

## The two judgment calls this plan resolves, and on what evidence

Both debug sessions flagged a value choice as needing a developer look. This is a solo-dev
gap-closure round, so each is decided here from the session's own measured decision space, with the
runner-up recorded so a retune is one number.

### `#cierre { padding-block }`: **5rem** (80px per side)

The session named **5rem-6rem** as the live range: below it, 4.5rem makes `#cierre` identical to
every other band and forfeits D-10's "distinct closing moment" intent, which plan 01.5-07's own
checkpoint explicitly declined to give up; above it, 7rem and 8rem keep the run an outlier.

5rem is chosen over 6rem on three measured grounds:

1. **Run against the page's own norm.** 5rem puts the inbound run at 152px against the page's
   measured 144px — a 5.6% step. 6rem leaves it at 168px, still 17% above norm, i.e. still visibly
   the odd boundary out on a page whose other three boundaries agree to the pixel.
2. **Distinctness is not carried by the padding.** `#cierre` is already the only band that is tinted
   AND has a `clamp(2rem, 4vw, 3rem)` heading. The session's own screenshot pair shows the band still
   reading as a deliberate tinted closing moment even at 4.5rem. 5rem keeps a real +8px-per-side step
   over the shared band padding on top of those two stronger signals.
3. **Asymmetric risk.** This band has now been reported as having too much empty space in two
   consecutive UAT rounds; it has never been reported as too tight. The cost of being 16px too tight
   is a fourth round on a minor complaint; the cost of being 16px too loose is a fourth round on the
   same major complaint the last two attempts already failed to close.

Recorded runner-up: **6rem** (run 168px, band 366px, 47.5% fill) if the end-of-phase walkthrough says
the band reads cramped. One value, one place.

### Symmetric padding is KEPT — the optical-centring option is declined, not overlooked

The session noted that because the two neighbours contribute unequal adjoining space (72px of band
padding above, a stripe below), the PERCEIVED runs are not symmetric even though the padding is, and
offered asymmetric `padding-block` as an option. It is declined here, deliberately:

- It would fail `checkCierreGapEvenness`'s 1px tolerance and require reopening plan 01.5-07's closed
  G-01.5-3 item 3b decision, whose whole point was making these two gaps equal.
- Task 1 of this plan removes the stripe below entirely, which changes the perceived-run arithmetic
  the option was reasoned from — it must be re-observed on the fixed page before being acted on.
- After Task 1 the band and the footer become one continuous tinted surface, so the band's bottom
  edge stops being a visual boundary at all; the space below the signature reads as leading into the
  footer rather than as trailing empty band.

The observation is carried forward in the SUMMARY so the next UAT round can raise it against the
fixed page instead of against the arithmetic of the broken one.

### The Cierre-to-footer target: **0px**, not a smaller budget

Not a judgment call — three independent measurements fix it (the page's four other boundaries are
0.00px; this is the only boundary whose surfaces match; the UAT truth asks for the band "flowing
directly into the footer"). Recorded here only so a reader does not mistake it for one.
