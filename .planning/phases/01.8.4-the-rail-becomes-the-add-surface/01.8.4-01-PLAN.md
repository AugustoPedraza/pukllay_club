above it can place at a **chosen** slot". The derived value (a member's `position`, today always
`max + 1`) becomes a chosen value.

- **Primary noun:** the **slot index** — a 0-based index into the gaps of the rendered rail
  (0 = before the first cover, n = after the last). The `section_games.position` column stays the
  storage detail it is today: dense `1..n`.
- **Decision: `add-alongside`.** `Sections.add_game/2` keeps its own `max + 1` append path
  untouched; `insert_game_at/3` lands beside it as the general positional writer. This is a locked
  user decision (RESEARCH.md § Resolved Open Questions, Q1: "Do not rewrite `add_game/2`'s
  `max + 1` or `repack_positions/1`'s base"), not a planner preference — `add_game/2` has four
  live callers (`edit.ex`, `index.ex`'s `undo-remove`, `set_game_sections/2`, and its own tests)
  and consolidating it was explicitly ruled out.
- **Accepted debt, and what would force a later promote:** two positional writers now share one
  table with two different position-assignment strategies (append-at-max vs renumber-from-order).
  A third positional writer, or any divergence where `add_game/2` leaves positions non-dense that
  `insert_game_at/3` then has to heal, forces the promote — at which point `add_game/2` becomes
  `insert_game_at(section, game_id, member_count)` and the `max + 1` path is deleted.
- **Invariant test encoding the generalized intent** (accepted, built as part of Task 2): after
  *any* sequence of `add_game/2`, `insert_game_at/3`, `move_game_to/3` and `remove_game/2` calls

  goes red the instant a future writer reintroduces a positional gap.

### Flagged assumptions (unclassified probe rows — planner assumptions, not acceptance criteria)

1. **CTX-02 / unclassified.** The probe could not classify "move-to-index excludes the moving
   game". The planner's assumption: the only edge worth pinning is the off-by-one, and it is
   pinned by Task 3's `rest_index/2` table (`[A,B,C,D]`: A to rest-index 2 → `[B,C,A,D]`; D to 0 →
   `[D,A,B,C]`; B to rest-index 1 → unchanged). If the developer expected a different semantic
   (for example an index into the list *with* the mover), say so before Task 3 lands — it changes
   the converter, not the storage.
2. **ADD-02 / unclassified.** The probe could not classify "the field is pinned and focused on
   open". The planner's assumption: `data-pk-sheet-autofocus` present in the rendered markup plus
   the `:commit`-slot placement is the automatable half; whether iOS actually raises the keyboard
   for a `focus()` issued after a LiveView round trip is **unverified on a real device**
   (RESEARCH.md assumption A3) and is routed to the end-of-phase device pass, not fixed
   speculatively.
3. **ADD-06 / unclassified.** The probe could not classify "placing closes the sheet and snacks
   `Juego agregado` + Deshacer". The planner's assumption: sheet-absence + snack text +
   `data-timeout="10000"` + a working `Deshacer` is the whole contract. **Known limitation carried
   into this phase (user-accepted, RESEARCH.md D6 / Q7):** `snackbar/1` renders `data-timeout` but
   **no JS consumer dismisses it**, so a no-action snack persists until something replaces it. No
   dismiss timer is built here. In scope instead: `clear_snackbars/1` clears the other snackbar
   assigns whenever one is set, so the new snacks can never stack with `@removed` or
   `@undo_snapshot`. **Follow-up for a later phase: wire a `data-timeout` consumer.**
