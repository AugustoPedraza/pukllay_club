<objective>
Give the add sheet its two reads: an accent-folded, starts-with-first ranked name search over every
non-retired game, and the `Últimas novedades` list of the 6 most recently added games not already
in the row.

Purpose: the sheet is only useful with real search — one offering just `Últimas novedades` would
leave staff unable to add anything outside the 6 newest games (the ROADMAP says exactly this is why
RAIL-05 and ADD-04 cannot be split apart). No ranked search exists anywhere in this codebase today:
every shipped name search is contains-only and un-ranked, so the `unaccent` + `CASE … ORDER BY`
query is genuinely new code.

Output: `Catalog.search_admin_games_ranked/2`, `Catalog.recent_games_for_row/2`, and the new
`test/pukllay_club/catalog/admin_game_search_test.exs` exercising both against the real test
database.
