### Flagged assumptions (unclassified probe rows — planner assumptions, not acceptance criteria)

1. **RAIL-03 / unclassified.** The probe could not classify "an empty row's rail is a single
   96×100 dashed tile beside the line". The planner's assumption: the instruction line lives
   **inside** the rail, beside the tile, which is why the empty-state rule sets
   `align-items: center` and `gap: 12px` on the rail (the findings note's own `.rail.empty` block).
   The already-shipped `Sin juegos, no se ve en el inicio.` paragraph stays where it is — a shipped
   test asserts it and CHROME-05 reworks it in phase 01.8.6 — so an empty row shows both lines
   until then. If the developer expected the two to be consolidated now, say so: that is a CHROME-05
   decision being pulled forward, not a defect here.
2. **RAIL-06 / unclassified.** The probe could not classify "a placed cover lands with a 520ms
   animation with a reduced-motion opt-out". The planner's assumption: the automatable contract is
   (a) the `--landed` class on exactly the right cover, (b) the declared duration and curve in the
   stylesheet source, and (c) the `no-preference` media-query containment. Whether the motion
   *reads* correctly, and whether it is genuinely absent with the OS "Reduce motion" setting on,
   are browser-only judgments and are routed to the end-of-phase device pass. One known mechanical
   caveat recorded by plan 01: `AdminRail` guards on `lastScrolledId`, so placing the **same** game
   twice in a row will not re-scroll it into view, even though it will re-animate.
