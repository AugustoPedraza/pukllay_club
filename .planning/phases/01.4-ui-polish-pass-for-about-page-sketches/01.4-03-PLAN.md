<objective>
Replace the closing band's duplicated WhatsApp + Instagram button pair with the single shared
"Sumate" CTA, and confirm — rather than assume — sketch 049's mobile CTA-bar centering claim
against the shipped code.

the footer, the drawer, and now (after plan 02) the rebuilt Contacto card directly above. 049's

destination across its three placements.

the two bands apart, and a recorded CDP verdict on the centering claim.
</objective>
