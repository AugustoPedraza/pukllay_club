<objective>
Close UAT gap G-01.4-3 (severity: cosmetic). After 01.4-06 gave the isologo mark its own `mb-8`
spacing tier, the user re-checked and said: *"That looks better but still the isologo should have
more space from bottom to improve balance and rythm."*

`.planning/debug/G-01.4-3-isologo-bottom-spacing.md` diagnoses this as three
independently-necessary conditions (AND-gate confirmed by live CDP measurement at five
viewport/theme combinations, a differential measurement of the approved sketch, and alpha-channel
profiling of both isologo PNGs):

1. **C1 MAGNITUDE.** `mb-8` (32px) is 16.0% of the 200px mark's own height. The approved design
   source — sketch 045 variant A3, the recorded winner — measures 78.5px below a 180px mark, i.e.
   43.6%. The shipped gap is 2.45x short absolutely and 2.7x short proportionally. The value was
   never derived from anything: 01.4-06 picked it one step past the top of `ui-design-system`'s
   documented spacing scale, which is a *text/section rhythm* scale (max `space-y-6` = 24px) with
   no tier at all for a display graphic.
2. **C2 INVERTED ALLOCATION.** The hero's `py-12` puts 48px *above* the mark and only 32px below
   it — measured identical at 375/414/768/1280px in both themes. The design source allocated 8px
   above and 78.5px below (below:above = 9.8:1) against the shipped 0.67:1, inverted roughly 15x.
   This is what makes the mark read as resting *on* the text rather than merely close to it, and
   it is what the user's word "balance" is pointing at. The sketch's clearance was the centring
   residual of a `min-height: 92vh; justify-content: center` hero; the implementation replaced that
   structure with a plain `py-12` block stack and never replaced the clearance the structure had
   been producing.
3. **C3 ZERO-CLEAR-SPACE ASSET.** Both isologo PNGs are tightly cropped — alpha ink reaches all
   four canvas edges at every threshold tested, and row coverage is still 31% of glyph width at
   90.6% down. The glyph donates 0px of optical padding, so the CSS margin is the *entire*
   perceived separation. A conventionally-padded brand lockup would have contributed 50-100px on
   its own at this size. This is a constraint the spacing decision must account for, not a defect
   to fix.

Compounding all three: the mark ships at 200px against an approved 180px (+11%), because 01.4-05
sized the anchor as "the 180px mark plus breathing room" but the mark was then sized *from* the
anchor rect and consumed it — and 01.4-06 welded `--pk-about-mark-h: 200px` to both the anchor
height and the mark height, so the anchor can no longer carry clearance by construction. The mark
is larger than approved (needing *more* clearance) while getting 2.45x less.

Purpose: 01.4-06's own correction ladder offered `mb-6`/`mb-10`/`mb-12` = 24/40/48px — a range
whose *largest* option is still 39% below the design source. Another turn of that ladder cannot
reach the right value, which is why this plan derives the number from the approved sketch instead
of guessing a step, and fixes the structural allocation and the anchor/mark weld alongside it.
All three of C1, C2 and C3 are addressed; none of them alone would close the gap.

Output: a hero whose isologo mark ships at its approved size, sits near the top of its own hero,
and carries a named, design-derived clear space below it that a future reader can trace.
</objective>
