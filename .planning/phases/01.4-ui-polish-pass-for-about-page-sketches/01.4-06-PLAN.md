<objective>
Close UAT gap G-01.4-1 (severity: major). The isologo scroll-morph shipped in plan 01.4-05 has
three independent defects, diagnosed to exact file:line in
`.planning/debug/G-01.4-1-isologo-morph-blink.md`:

- **S1 BLINK** — the header ships fully visible from the server and is hidden only by client JS
  after the LiveSocket join, and that hide is itself a 280ms animated opacity fade. The visitor
  sees a solid header dissolve away.
- **S2 SPACING** — the 200px mark anchor is separated from the kicker line by the hero's flat
  12px `space-y-3`, the same gap a 12px text line gets. Proximity is switched off.
- **S3 NOT FLUID** — three contributors, all present: `--ease-out-soft` on layout properties
  over a 160-517px travel (32.7% of the move lands in the first frame), an undock transition
  cancelled one frame later by `.no-anim`, and scroll tracking gated behind the 500ms entrance
  timer.

Purpose: the developer asked for "a subtle polished professional animation", and the current
mechanism cannot produce one — its architecture (CSS transition on `top/left/width/height`,
re-placed every frame with a forced reflow) is what generates all three motion defects.

Output: a header that is hidden from the server's first byte, a mark with its own spacing tier,
and a morph driven by per-frame transform interpolation against two live rects, so no target is
ever stale and no transition can be cancelled.
</objective>
