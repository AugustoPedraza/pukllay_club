<objective>
Rebuild the About page's Contacto section as the single merged card sketch 048 locked: real icon
links resolved through the codebase's one shared `social_links/1` definition, plus a static
Google Maps thumbnail linking out to the club's real venue location.

Purpose: today's Contacto block is two bare text links ("WhatsApp · Instagram") with no icons and
no location — a visitor cannot see where the club actually is without leaving the page. 048's
finding is that a card next to a separately-sized map thumbnail breaks the page's rhythm, so the
links and the map must live inside one card sharing one edge.

Output: a new `ClubLinks.maps_url/0`, a promoted and parameterized `Layouts.social_links/1`, a
committed Maps screenshot asset, the rebuilt card markup, its About-scoped CSS, and tests
covering both the new card and the unchanged footer/drawer call sites.
</objective>
