<objective>
Finish closing verification gap **G-01.4-5** by making Google's attribution actually *readable*,
then have a human confirm the whole component — the step whose absence let this gap ship.

Plan 01.4-10 stopped the crop from discarding the wordmark. That is necessary and it is not
sufficient. The asset is 1656px wide and the thumbnail renders at 299-538 CSS px, a 3.1-5.5x
downscale, so the baked-in "Google Maps" wordmark — 18 source pixels tall — arrives on screen at
**3.3 CSS px at 375px, 2.5 at 640px, 3.1 at 768px and 5.9 at 1280px**. Present in the pixel
buffer; unreadable by a person. This is the same downscaling arithmetic
`.planning/debug/G-01.4-4-maps-thumbnail-approach.md` already used to condemn the previous
capture's map labels at "2.7-4.9 CSS px", and it applies to Google's own mark just as squarely.

Google's Geo Guidelines settle what to do about it. They require that you *"Don't remove, obscure,
or crop out the attribution information"* — 01.4-10's job — and they then say: *"If necessary, you
may customize the style and placement of the attribution text, as long as the text is within close
proximity of the content and legible to the average viewer or reader."* At 2.5 CSS px the baked-in
mark is not legible to the average viewer, so the customization clause is not an optional
embellishment here, it is the remaining half of the requirement 01.4-09-PLAN.md called "a hard
requirement, not a preference" and that `01.4-VERIFICATION.md` truth 10 states as *"as Google
Maps' terms require"*.

This project already knows how to do this. `bgg_attribution/1` and `.pk-bgg-note` render a
third-party credit as real DOM text adjacent to the content it credits, with a comment block
explaining exactly why the mark's presentation is not altered casually. The map gets the same
treatment.

And then a person looks at it. `01.4-UAT.md` marks G-01.4-3 and G-01.4-4 `resolved` with no
recorded post-fix human re-check; `STATE.md`, timestamped after both plans landed, still reads
"Phase 01.4 plans complete pending end-of-phase UAT"; and this pass's own screenshot inspection
immediately found a concrete defect that such a check would have caught. This component has been
measured mechanically correct and rejected on sight **twice**. Its human check is not deferred
again here.

Purpose: satisfy the attribution requirement in the way it is actually written — legibly, in close
proximity — and break this phase's habit of closing map-thumbnail gaps without anyone looking.

Output: a readable attribution credit under the Contacto map, gated by tests and by the live-render
probe, and a recorded human confirmation of the finished component at all four widths in both
themes.
</objective>
