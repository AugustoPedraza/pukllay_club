<objective>
Close verification gap **G-01.4-5** the way the reopened decision says to close it: stop hand-
engineering the legibility of a screenshot's baked-in attribution, and let Google render its own.

The gap's whole history is one component fighting one image. 01.4-07 shrink-wrapped a caption chip
that was covering the map. 01.4-09 recaptured the screenshot at a legible zoom. 01.4-10 discovered
that `.pk-about-map-thumb`'s `aspect-ratio` still described an asset 01.4-09 had already replaced,
so `object-fit: cover` was silently discarding Google's wordmark off the bottom edge at every
breakpoint. 01.4-11 then added a `Datos del mapa © Google` figcaption because, even uncropped, an
18-source-pixel wordmark on a 1656px asset displayed at 299-538 CSS px arrives on screen 2.5-5.9
CSS px tall — present in the pixel buffer, unreadable by a person.

Every one of those was correct given the premise. D-11 removes the premise. A live embed paints its
own attribution at its own native size, in a frame Google controls, and the legibility problem
stops being ours to solve.

`.planning/debug/resolved/G-01.4-4-maps-thumbnail-approach.md` recorded — with a live headless-Chrome
repro — that the embed is blocked today: `securitypolicyviolation` with `effectiveDirective`
`frame-src`, `blockedURI` `https://www.google.com`, and the browser naming the `default-src 'self'`
fallback explicitly. That finding is still true and it is exactly why the first task here is a
tracer: the architectural risk in this plan is not the markup, it is whether the frame actually
commits once the policy permits it. Prove that end-to-end in a real browser before building
anything on top of it.

Two things this plan is honest about that the source artifacts are not:

1. **Plan 01.4-11 shipped.** D-15 says "not executed"; commits `a85047c` and `d2b771e` say
   otherwise. Its figcaption, its ExUnit gates and its probe assertions are live code, and undoing
   them is real work in Tasks 1 and 3.
2. **The keyless `?pb=` payload is undocumented and unsupported.** Google can change it and the
   map goes blank with no build failure and no test failure — there is no server-side signal at
   all. The rebuilt probe in Task 3 is the only detector this repo will have, which is why it
   gates the child frame's actual navigation commit rather than merely the presence of an
   `<iframe>` tag in the markup.

Purpose: make the Contacto map a real map, under a policy that permits exactly one frame origin and
no more, without handing visitors a scroll-trap.

Output: a live, non-interactive, dark-theme-approximated Google Maps embed behind a single click-out
link; a `frame-src` directive derived from the embed URL itself; the screenshot pipeline and its
pixel oracle deleted; an 8-case browser probe; and a recorded human verdict on the dark-theme
approximation D-13 says only a person can settle.
</objective>
