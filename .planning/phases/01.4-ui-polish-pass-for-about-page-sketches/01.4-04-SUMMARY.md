## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] Fixed the carousel's last-dot scroll-math mismatch**
- **Found during:** Task 3 (live Chrome verification of dot navigation)
- **Issue:** `goTo(index)` always scrolled to `index * slideAdvance()`, but the rail cannot scroll past its content edge — once slide count grew, the last dot's target (2936px) exceeded the rail's real max `scrollLeft` (2455px). The browser silently clamped the scroll, and `onScroll`'s `round(scrollLeft / advance)` math then computed the wrong index (3 instead of 4), so the 5th dot never highlighted and clicking it landed on the 4th slide instead. Confirmed via measurement this was a pre-existing formula flaw (not specific to 5 slides) that had likely also affected the 4th (then-last) dot before this plan, just unobserved.
- **Fix:** Added `maxScrollLeft()` and routed the last index through it in both `goTo` (scroll target) and `onScroll` (index detection near the boundary), leaving all other indices' math untouched.
- **Files modified:** `lib/pukllay_club_web/live/about_live.ex`
- **Verification:** CDP script confirmed before/after: before the fix, clicking `[data-goto="4"]` landed on `activeDot: "3"`; after, `isActive: true, ariaCurrent: "true"` on the 5th dot, with autoplay and pause-on-hover unaffected.
- **Committed in:** `f097241`
