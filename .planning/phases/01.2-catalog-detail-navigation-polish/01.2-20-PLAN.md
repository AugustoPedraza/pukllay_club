<objective>
Remove the duplicated dificultad block from the detail page's reading column, move the filter link it
owned onto the pill that survives, and give the Mecánicas/Temáticas chips a border and background that
actually read against the page.

The reading column currently shows the game's difficulty twice: once as a compact pill in the facts
row, and again lower down as a large badge plus an explanatory sentence. A prior round deliberately
kept that second copy, reasoning that the pill was a summary and the badge was the explanation. The
next real UAT pass reversed it in one line: "still it shows its 'category' pills with a
description(remove it)". Sketch 034 removed it in every variant it explored.

The consequence hiding behind that removal is that the badge was wrapped in the page's only link into
the weight-band filter, and the facts row's own dificultad pill was left deliberately unlinked
*because* the badge owned that target. Removing one without moving the link to the other quietly
drops a filter entry point.

The second half of the same UAT line — "the 'labesl' and 'values' for the mecanicas, tematicas isn't
well balanced(has border to together to text and background don't constraint)" — is a plain styling
gap: those chips render with the stock component default and have never had a rule of their own in
this stylesheet.

Purpose: one fact, one home, and chips a reader can actually see the edges of.
Output: the badge block gone, the filter link relocated, and one new scoped chip rule.
</objective>
