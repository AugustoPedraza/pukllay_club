<objective>
Make the lightbox's backdrop actually opaque, so the overlay covers the whole screen.

Round 7 (01.2-29) grew the photo stage to the full HEIGHT of the screen and closed the vertical
bands. The user then reported the same complaint again, now horizontal: at 1920px a light-lavender
band fills the sides of the window beside the stage, and at 390px a thin sliver survives at the
edges.

The first automated diagnosis blamed dev-mode live reload — a missing `:live_reload` `patterns`
block in `config/dev.exs` — and concluded the CSS was already correct and the user was simply
looking at a stale tab. That is DISPROVEN. The block exists in `config/runtime.exs` at line 45 and
has since the scaffold commit `f432d98` (`git log --reverse -S'live_reload' -- config/runtime.exs`),
and STATE.md independently records plan `01-02` fixing a regex bug in those very patterns. The
original session read one config file, found no key, and reached for an environmental explanation
without reconciling it against the geometry.

The geometry is the actual cause, and it reconciles exactly. `.pk-lightbox-img` is capped at
`--pk-shell-content-width`, which at a 1920px window resolves to roughly 1216px — leaving roughly
704px, about 37% of the window, of `.pk-lightbox`'s own background exposed laterally; at 390px the
gutter subtraction leaves a thin sliver per side. A wide band at desktop and a sliver at mobile is
precisely what the two screenshots show. And that exposed background is not the page showing
through a hole: `.pk-lightbox` is `position: fixed; inset: 0` and has always covered the entire
viewport. What is visible around the stage is this element's own 72% scrim compositing over the
page beneath it.

So the fix is one property on the surface that already had the right geometry. `.pk-lightbox`'s
background becomes a bare, full-strength read of `--pk-shadow-color` — the same token, at the same
strength, that `.pk-lightbox-img`'s stage already reads — and the container and the stage become
one continuous dark field by construction rather than by coincidence.

The photo stage itself does not change size. Growing it to `100vw` was the other option and was
rejected: its width reads `--pk-shell-content-width`, the same boundary both chevron inset rules
read, and 01.2-28's chevron/stage alignment was measured and signed off at 1920px. Widening the
child to fix a paint problem would silently move a boundary two other rules depend on.

`--pk-overlay-scrim` is not re-tuned and not deleted. Its value is byte-identical, because
`.pk-sheet-backdrop` still needs exactly 72%. The lightbox needs 100%, which is a DIFFERENT value,
not a new setting for the same one — so the two surfaces split by reader, and the token's own
comment about how many readers it has gets corrected in the same commit.

Purpose: close G-01.2-19 — severity major, the third and last report of "the background should be
full screen", one property deep.
Output: an overlay that covers the screen at every viewport, a re-pointed test that proves the
container reads the opaque token while a separate assertion proves the scrim token's value survives
intact for the sheet that still consumes it, and the container-not-child lesson recorded beside the
round-6 and round-7 lessons it completes.
</objective>
