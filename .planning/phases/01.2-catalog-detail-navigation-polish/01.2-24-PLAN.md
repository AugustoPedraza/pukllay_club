<objective>
Stop a phone-only bar from appearing on desktop, stop a long game name from wrapping inside it, put a
real gap back between the last carousel and the footer, and stop the footer from floating above the
bottom of a tall window.

UAT tests 9 and 13 reported the same four things from two directions: "on desktop I see a kind of
floating header with title name that is being showed on scrolling (that is a behaviour only for
mobile)"; "don't have correct balance to contrast with background (looks like text over two lines)";
"not margin between the carousel of juegos similares and the start of the footer"; and "a weird spacing
between the footer content and the window bottom".

All four were reproduced live against the dev server with real measurements. Three are small and
local. The fourth is not a detail-page defect at all: this app has never had a sticky-footer layout,
and the detail page simply became the first page short enough for that to show, because the previous
gap-closure round trimmed its padding.

Purpose: the page should end where the window ends, with one deliberate gap before the footer, and the
condensed title bar should exist only where it was designed to.
Output: a breakpoint-scoped hide, a single-line title, corrected boundary arithmetic, and a
sticky-footer layout the whole app gets for free.
</objective>
