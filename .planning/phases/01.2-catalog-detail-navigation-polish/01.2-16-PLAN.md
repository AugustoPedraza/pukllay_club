<objective>
Give the catalog's search/filter pipeline a real in-flight and settle affordance, so results stop
teleporting on production latency.

`handle_event("search", ...)` → `apply_filters/1` → `stream(:games, games, reset: true)` has zero
loading-state or transition wiring today. Three separate absences combine into one symptom: no
client-side in-flight indicator is ever set for this request path (unlike `.GridScroll`, which does
set `data-loading` around its own `pushEvent`); the three `@custom-variant phx-*-loading` rules
declared at the top of `app.css` are applied to no selector anywhere; and `.pk-card`/the results
containers carry no animatable channel at all, so even a class-based fade would have nothing to
animate. Invisible on local dev, jarring on a real network — which is precisely why the UAT
response called out the environment difference explicitly.

Purpose: the catalog is the product's browse surface, and "I typed and the page blinked" is the
difference between a search box that feels alive and one that feels broken.
Output: one in-flight signal wired from LiveView's own page-loading events, a branded progress bar
instead of stock scaffolding blue, and a fade on both the card stream reset and the surface flip.
</objective>
