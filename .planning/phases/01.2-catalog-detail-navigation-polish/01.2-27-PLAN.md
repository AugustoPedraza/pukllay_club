<objective>
Finish the consolidation: put the catalog page's two remaining chip families on the base the previous
plan built, and lock the door behind them.

The UAT's question had four parts — the facts pills, the mechanic/theme chips, the "búsqueda activa"
chips, and the filter modal's chips. The previous plan built the base and moved the first two. This plan
moves the last two and adds the gate that stops a sixth family from appearing.

The interesting part is not the migration; it is a comment. The active-filter chip's rule states that it
and the modal's chip "must NEVER be merged: a removable applied-filter chip and a selectable modal option
chip are different affordances, even sharing the same base pill shape." That comment is right about the
affordances and it already concedes the shared shape. Both statements survive here: the two remain
visibly different, as two tones of one component. What does not survive is two hand-maintained rule sets
that happened to converge. The comment gets rewritten to say what is now true rather than left standing
as a contradiction someone will one day resolve in the wrong direction.

Purpose: five families, one representation, and a test that fails when a sixth appears.
Output: two migrated call sites, three retired rule bodies, and a drift gate with a named exclusion.
</objective>
