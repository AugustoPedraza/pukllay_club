<objective>
Restructure the detail-page masthead so the facts pills sit above the poster instead of on top of it,
the Reservar button sits outside the poster's bordered panel instead of inside it, the gallery dots
are centered, and the whole masthead uses the same width the header and footer use.

This is the structural half of both UAT gaps at once. G-01.2-11 (mobile) and G-01.2-12 (desktop) are
two reports against the same shipped masthead, and sketch 032 answered both with one layout: pills
move from an absolute overlay to a plain in-flow row above the panel, the panel's bordered/shadowed
treatment moves off the column and onto an inner element so the CTA can step outside it, and the
page's own narrower content cap is dropped in favour of the shell's.

There is no leading tracer task in this plan. A tracer proves an unproven architecture end to end;
this page is already wired end to end, shipped, and UAT'd twice. What is unproven here is a layout,
and the way to prove a layout is to build it and look at it, which is what Task 1 plus its human
check does.

Purpose: a reader arriving on a phone should see the game's facts, then its cover, then one clear
Reservar — three separate things, in that order — and a reader on a desktop should see a page whose
edges line up with the header above it.
Output: one facts row instead of two, one bordered panel that no longer swallows the CTA, centered
dots, and a masthead that shares the shell's width.
</objective>
