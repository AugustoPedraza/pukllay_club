<objective>

carry data, de-duplicate players/duration, and give the buy-box the fuller redesign pass the
user asked for — implementing the resolutions `01.2-UI-SPEC.md` already made, not re-opening
them.

for fields that structurally can never populate, and repeats players/duration a few hundred
pixels below the pills that already state them. The buy-box, meanwhile, is the page's one
decision surface but renders bare, at the wrong aspect ratio for box art, with its primary
action visually tied for weight with a share button. All three are fixed here.
Output: a shorter, honest reading column and a buy-box that reads as a self-contained panel.
