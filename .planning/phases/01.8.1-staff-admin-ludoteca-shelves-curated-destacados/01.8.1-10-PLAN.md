## Phase Goal

**As a** club staff member (the owner or one of up to 3 invited staff), **I want to** sign in and
manage the ludoteca, record where each physical game lives, curate the home page's sections, and fix
mis-banded games, **so that** Saturday operations run smoothly and the home page stays alive.

<objective>
Replace the eight hardcoded carousel rows with database-driven sections, migrate today's hashtag
rows into them with their members, and render a hand-picked featured section as the home hero.

Purpose: SC-4 as rewritten by CONTEXT (managed sections supersede "rename the first carousel").
Every later section feature — staff management (plan 12) and the sections filter facet (plan 11) —
needs the sections model and the home page reading from it first.

<assumption_delta_decision>
Noun now primary: **Section** (a database row with a kind: manual, weight_band or recent).
Decision: **promote** — sections replace both the hardcoded `carousel_row_specs/0` keys and the
three editorial hashtags as the home page's organizing concept; `weight_band` is demoted to the
membership rule of one section kind rather than a separate row type, and hashtags stop being a
live concept (their data is migrated, and the checkpoint below decides what happens to the column).
Rationale: D-17 makes rows staff-owned and many-to-many; keeping hashtags alongside as a second
live source of membership would let the two silently disagree.
</assumption_delta_decision>

Output: user decision on `games.tags`; `sections`/`section_games` + D-22 backfill;
`Catalog.Section`, `Catalog.SectionGame`; `Catalog.list_home_sections/0` and `section_page/3`;
`CatalogLive.Index` rendering sections with the featured hero.
</objective>
