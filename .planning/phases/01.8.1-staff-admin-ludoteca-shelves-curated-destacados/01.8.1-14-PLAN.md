## Phase Goal

**As a** club staff member (the owner or one of up to 3 invited staff), **I want to** sign in and
manage the ludoteca, record where each physical game lives, curate the home page's sections, and fix
mis-banded games, **so that** Saturday operations run smoothly and the home page stays alive.

<objective>
Ship the phase to pukllay.club and prove, against the live host, the things a green test suite
cannot: the one-way migrations left the real catalog intact, real email reaches a real inbox, the
owner can sign in, and production enrichment works with real credentials.

Purpose: RESEARCH Pitfall 1 is explicit that a fixture-based suite never catches a migration
default bug against pre-existing production rows; D-36's done bar is a real Gmail delivery. Both
need the deployed system.

Output: a reusable production smoke script, a pre-deploy baseline, the merged and deployed phase,
the owner account, and recorded live verification.
</objective>
