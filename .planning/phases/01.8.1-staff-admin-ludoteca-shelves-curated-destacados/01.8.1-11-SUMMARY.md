**Resolution:** Task 2 is committed as a single `feat(01.8.1-11): ...` commit (`99b5603`) containing both the new tests and their implementation, rather than a red/green pair. No missing-RED violation is recorded because a meaningful RED state was not reachable without first landing the (non-behavioral) schema change — the same "investigated, feature-shape prerequisite" resolution 01.8.1-10 documented for its own Task 3.
