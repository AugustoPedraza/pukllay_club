## Phase Goal

**As a** club staff member (the owner or one of up to 3 invited staff), **I want to** sign in and
manage the ludoteca, record where each physical game lives, curate the home page's sections, and fix
mis-banded games, **so that** Saturday operations run smoothly and the home page stays alive.

<objective>
Give staff control of the home page's sections: rename, reorder, hide, choose each section's sort
rule, create new hand-picked sections, and pick and order their games from a phone.

Purpose: SC-4 as rewritten (D-17..D-26). Plan 10 made the home page read sections; this plan is
where the owner actually curates them — including the featured hero and a future "Spiel des Jahres".

Output: `Section` changesets, `Catalog.Sections`, `Admin.SectionLive.Index` and
`Admin.SectionLive.Edit`, section checkboxes on the game form, and the dashboard Secciones card.
</objective>
