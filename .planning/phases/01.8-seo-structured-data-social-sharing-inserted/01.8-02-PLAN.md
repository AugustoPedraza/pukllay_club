<objective>
Give the catalog card and the hover-preview cover art a real accessible name — game name plus
publisher, per D-10 — across all three render branches each component has, not just the happy-path
`<img>`.

Purpose: SEO-02 and ROADMAP success criterion 5 both name the broken-image case explicitly. This
app currently renders three independently-gated cover branches per component and none of them has
an accessible name: the `<img>` carries an empty alt, and the two placeholder `<div>`s are
icon-only. A fix applied only to the `<img>` passes a happy-path screen-reader spot-check while
leaving the two failure branches silent.

Output: a shared `PukllayClubWeb.GameText` generator handling the sparse-publishers case, applied
to all six render branches, plus the moduledoc correction recording why the earlier empty-alt
decision was reversed.
</objective>
