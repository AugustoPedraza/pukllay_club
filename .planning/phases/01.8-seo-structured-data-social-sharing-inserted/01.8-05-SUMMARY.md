**2. [Rule 1 - Bug] Two pre-existing byte-identical-markup assertions broke because they didn't normalize the new per-request JSON-LD nonce**
- **Found during:** Task 1, running the full `test/pukllay_club_web/` suite as an additional regression check beyond the plan's own listed commands
