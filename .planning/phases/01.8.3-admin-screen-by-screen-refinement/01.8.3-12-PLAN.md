Purpose: `juegos.css:339` sets the pinned band's `bottom: calc(var(--pt) - 2 * var(--bandp))`. With
`--bandp = (44px - 18.2px) / 2 = 12.9px` (`juegos.css:226`) this resolves to `--pt - 25.8px` —
NEGATIVE for any `--pt` below 25.8px. The first section declares `--pt: 14px` (`juegos.css:234`), so
`bottom = -11.8px`: the sticky header reserves a 32.2px box while painting a 44px opaque band. 11.8px
of tint lies OUTSIDE its own sticky box, covering flow content that NO scroll-offset fix can
compensate. Measured on the first section pinned at 390x844: `headerBox h=32.2` against `band h=44`,
`bandOverhangBelowStickyBox = +11.8px`, and the row "bot factory" (64px tall) hidden 39.7px behind the
band with 0px visible below it. Later sections (`--pt: 26px`) resolve to +0.2px and do not overhang.
This is exactly the section the developer's prose named — "the band that is pinted for borradores".

Plan 11 closed the primary cause (the static sticky offset against a dynamic row) and deliberately
left this one, per the UAT's carried constraint that the two must not be folded together. Plan 11's
GREEN transcript records the residual overhang as a measured number so this plan's guard can be
negative-tested against a known quantity.

Output: a band that never exceeds its host's own box, the developer's choice of which of the two
available shapes achieves it, the source-level tripwire kept binding, and a guard recorded failing on
the first section while passing on a later one.

## The geometry admits exactly two shapes, and neither is free

For 44px of opaque tint at the top of the viewport not to cover anything it did not displace, the
pinned header must RESERVE 44px of flow. The header's box today is `--pt + --cap-box`, which is 32.2px
for the first section and 44.2px for every other. The only free choice is where the missing 11.8px
goes, and each choice costs something specific:

- **Put it above the ink, at rest as well as pinned.** The first section's `--pt` rises to at least
  `2 x --bandp` (25.8px). In practice this means dropping the `:first-child` override entirely so the
  first section uses the base rule's `--pt: 26px` like every other section — no new literal, no new
  derivation. Its box becomes 44.0px at rest and 44.0px pinned, its band `bottom` resolves to +0.2px
  exactly like a later section's, and the pinned box height stops depending on `--pt` at all. Cost:
  this REVERSES D-19g-bis decision 21's deliberate first-section exception ("the first section takes
  less leading air — the search/add block above it already separates it"), adding ~12px of resting air
  between the search row and «Borradores».
- **Grow the box only while pinned.** Make the pinned rule's padding independent of `--pt`:
  `padding-top: calc(var(--bandp) + var(--cap))` unchanged, `padding-bottom: calc(var(--bandp) -
  var(--cap))`. The pinned box becomes `2 x --bandp + --cap-box` = 44.0px for every section, and the
  band's `bottom` becomes 0. `--pt: 14px` survives and the resting layout is untouched. Cost: the
  first section's header box grows from 32.2px to 44.0px at the instant it pins, so every row below it
  shifts down ~11.8px on pin and back up on unpin — a visible jitter at one scroll position. Later
  sections are unaffected (44.2 to 44.0).

What both share: the band's own top, height and background do not move in either shape, so the ink
invariants are arithmetically untouched. What neither can do: keep `--pt: 14px`, keep the resting
layout, keep the 44px band AND avoid a pin-moment shift. That combination is over-determined.

## The invariants, stated as the code actually enforces them

The prompt-level constraint "keep `inkGapAbove == inkGapBelow` (13.5/13.5)" is a paraphrase and must
not be implemented literally. What `admin_shell.mjs` actually enforces, and what must survive:

- `checkInkInBand`: both gaps non-null, both strictly greater than 0, and
  `|gapAbove - gapBelow| <= 2.0px` per section. The later section's shipped pair is NOT exactly equal.
- The cross-section gate: `|gapAbove_first - gapAbove_later| <= 1.0px`. THIS is the comparison whose
  log line reads `first=13.5px later=13.5px delta=0px`.
- Band height 44.0 ± 0.5 per section, and cross-section band-height delta within 0.5px.

Do not tighten either tolerance to chase a number that was never shipped. Do not relax either.
