<objective>
Delete `AdminComponents.page_bar/1` and every piece of scaffolding that existed only to serve it —
the component, its four CSS rule blocks, and its assertions in three test files plus the visual
harness (D-09).

itself so the codebase carries no orphan, and repairs the four test files that assert it exists. No
planned screen wants a page bar — Estantes and Web will drop their titles too, and the five admin
sub-screens use `back_row/1` without one.

`back_row/1` demonstrably untouched.

**Phase prohibition inventory:** six prohibitions were recalled for this phase. Four are authored in
plan 01 (accessible page name, disabled-control ground, no `phx-value-value`, voseo), one here (no
geometry change outside `/admin/juegos`), one in plan 03 (status is a dot + text, never a pill). One
candidate was dropped by canon referral rather than minted: client-forged `phx-value-*` edition ids
are canon security — covered by `/gsd-secure-phase` and by T-01.8.3-01 in plans 01 and 04.
