# Phase 01.8.3 Plan 06: Pinned Caption Ink-in-Band Fix Summary

**A two-declaration pinned-padding override centres the Juegos section caption's ink inside its 44px pinned band for every section, backed by a probe assertion observed failing on the live defect and a browser-free ExUnit tripwire.**

- **Tasks:** 3

## Accomplishments

- Fixed the shipped pinned-band defect: Borradores (`--pt: 14px`) read 14px above / 13px below its caption ink (centred by coincidence), while Juegos del club (`--pt: 26px`) read 26px above / 1px below (25px of asymmetry, ink flush against the band's bottom edge). - Closed `01.8.3-UAT.md`'s deferred `loginAsStaff` cold-boot flake follow-up by replacing its fixed 1200ms sleep with a `pollUntil` poll against the same tab-bar presence check.

## Decisions Made

- Moved the ink via a pinned-state `padding-top`/`padding-bottom` pair on the header itself rather than the UAT's own literal example remedy (`top: calc(var(--pt) - var(--bandp))` on the band), which the plan's own objective explains would have re-broken the flush-adjacency invariant 01.8.3-05 shipped on purpose.
- Carried the `--cap` optical nudge into the new `padding-top` declaration (not left on the band), so the deliberate cap-height correction (071 d29-31) still applies to where the ink sits now that the band's own geometry is fixed at `top: 0`.
- The ExUnit gate parses `--pt` from the whole stripped stylesheet (not one rule), so the tight `:first-child` override — 13.5px of headroom above the computed floor — is covered by the precondition test, not just the base rule's looser value.
